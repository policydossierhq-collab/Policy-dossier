Soldiers earn peanuts, APC youth leader flaunts billion-naira SUVs — Utomi

2026-10-25

Professor Pat Utomi has criticised the All Progressives Congress (APC) after its national youth leader, Dayo Israel, displayed new Toyota Prado SUVs that he said were presented to selected youth leaders. Utomi questioned the vehicles’ cost and the priorities behind the distribution, contrasting it with what he described as low salaries for Nigerian soldiers.

In a post on X on Sunday, Utomi said it was troubling that an APC youth leader was showing off expensive vehicles while Nigerians had been told government funds were limited. He wrote that soldiers were being paid “peanuts” and argued the situation could leave the public feeling they were being treated as fools.

Utomi also raised the possibility of using the money for other needs, particularly health services. He said each SUV in the video cost more than ₦100 million and asked how many “world-class ambulances” the expenditure could buy. The cost figure was presented by Utomi; the source material does not give an overall price for the vehicles or identify who paid for them.

Israel said the vehicles were intended to support election mobilisation ahead of the 2027 general elections. In his post accompanying the video, he thanked First Lady Oluremi Tinubu for what he described as her continued support for the party’s structure under his leadership, and said the party would work to deliver “polling unit by polling unit.” The video has since been deleted. Its display prompted criticism on social media, including questions about the timing of the distribution amid economic hardship associated with the administration’s reforms. Utomi’s comments added to those concerns by focusing on public spending priorities.
