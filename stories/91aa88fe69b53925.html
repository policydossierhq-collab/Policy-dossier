Security threats: Army chief urges troops to remain vigilant

2026-10-30

Nigerian Army Chief of Staff Lt. Gen. Waidi Shaibu has urged personnel to remain vigilant, disciplined and committed as they respond to changing security threats. He delivered the message on Thursday at the inauguration of projects at Adekunle Fajuyi and Letmauck Cantonments in Ibadan, Oyo State. Shaibu was represented at the event by Maj. Gen. Auwalu Mahmuda, General Officer Commanding 2 Division.

The projects at Adekunle Fajuyi Cantonment include two 10-person transit accommodation units for corporals and below, five blocks of family quarters for the same ranks, and two houses for regimental sergeants major serving with the 2 Military Intelligence Brigade and 502 Signal Brigade. At Letmauck Cantonment in Mokola, the works include the 81 Battalion Officers’ Mess, sports facilities and semi-detached three-bedroom married officers’ quarters.

Shaibu said personnel should demonstrate discipline, loyalty, integrity, selfless service and professionalism, and maintain high standards of conduct and accountability. He also called on 81 Battalion to preserve its operational effectiveness, work with other security agencies and respond to emerging threats within the division’s area of responsibility. He said the Army was seeking to develop a professional, combat-ready and resilient force amid security challenges across the country.

The Army chief described the new facilities as investments in personnel welfare and operational readiness, saying suitable living conditions could support morale and unit cohesion. He called on beneficiaries to take responsibility for maintaining the buildings and supporting facilities, warning that neglect could undermine their purpose. He also reaffirmed the Army’s commitment to personnel welfare and recognition of troops’ service to national security.

Brigade Commander Uchechukwu Okoroji said the structures had been a welfare concern before renovation and remodelling, and that the improvements could strengthen morale and commitment to duty. 81 Battalion Commanding Officer Lt. Col. Chiemezie Asogwa thanked Shaibu for approving and facilitating the work, describing it as part of efforts to improve conditions for personnel and their families.
