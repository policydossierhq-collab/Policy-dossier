Shooting At California Halloween Store Kills One, Wounds Six

2026-10-31

One person was killed and six others were wounded in a shooting Friday evening at a Spirit Halloween store in Fairfield, northern California, police said. The shooting took place shortly before 8:30 p.m. at the store in the Solano Town Center mall, about 47 miles northeast of San Francisco.

Fairfield Police Chief Dan Marshall said the six people wounded by gunfire were taken to hospital. At least three were in critical condition. Police had not identified the person or people responsible, and Marshall said the crime scene and investigation remained active late Friday.

The shooting occurred hours before Halloween, which was being celebrated in the United States on Saturday. Marshall said areas outside the mall were not under lockdown.
