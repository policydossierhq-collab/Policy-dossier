Grand Theft Auto VI Takes Record $3.8 bn On First day

2026-11-20

Grand Theft Auto VI generated an estimated $3.79 billion in worldwide retail sales on its first day, according to Rockstar Games. The estimate covers sales through the end of Thursday. Rockstar described the launch as the biggest for an entertainment product in history. The total is nearly five times the roughly $800 million that Grand Theft Auto V earned in its first 24 hours in 2013.

The game had sold 32.5 million units before release, a record across all platforms, the company said. It launched Thursday for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, beginning at midnight in New Zealand and becoming available in other markets as midnight arrived locally. Monitoring platform Visibrain reported that the release prompted more social media posts than this year’s World Cup.

Reviews have been highly positive. The game has a score of 99 on Metacritic, which Rockstar called the highest ever for a game. It received eight nominations for the Game Awards, scheduled for December 10. Rockstar spent more than eight years developing the title and delayed its launch twice, first from autumn 2025 and later from May 2026.

The release follows a 2022 hack in which stolen gameplay footage was posted online. The series has also faced longstanding criticism over its depiction of violence and crime, including gameplay in which players can run over pedestrians, sell drugs and shoot at police. Rockstar has no official release planned in Russia, China, Lebanon or Qatar.
