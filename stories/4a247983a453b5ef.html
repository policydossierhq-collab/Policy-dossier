Uyo Book Club plans colloquium on Attah’s 88th birthday

2026-11-16

Uyo Book Club will hold a colloquium at 11 a.m. on Friday to mark former Akwa Ibom State governor Victor Attah’s 88th birthday. The event, at Watbridge Hotels and Suites in Uyo, will focus on the theme “Attah’s Vision in the Aviation Sector” and examine his contribution to the state’s aviation development. It will be free and open to the public.

The club’s founder, Udeme Nana, announced the event. Nana, a communication scholar and former head of the Department of Mass Communication at Akwa Ibom State Polytechnic, served as Attah’s spokesperson from 1999 to 2007. He said the gathering would consider the former governor’s vision for aviation infrastructure and its continuing effects.

Attah governed Akwa Ibom from 1999 to 2007. He conceived the state airport project and began construction before leaving office. Successive administrations completed the project and continued to develop the airport, which is named after him. It has since been expanded and includes a terminal building and a Maintenance, Repair and Overhaul facility.

Expected speakers include Mfon Udom, chairman of Ibom Air’s board; former Niger Delta Affairs Minister Umana Okon Umana; Ita Awak, a former director of airworthiness standards at the Nigerian Civil Aviation Authority; and Augustine Okon, former chief executive of the defunct ADC Airline. They are expected to discuss the airport’s conception, development and significance, as well as the roles played by people involved in the project.

The colloquium is part of Uyo Book Club’s annual events recognising influential figures. The organisation also hosts reading sessions and literary discussions, and says its community has more than 7,000 members. It has previously held colloquia honouring Nigerian writers and journalists, including Ken Saro-Wiwa, John Pepper Clark, Wole Soyinka and Chinua Achebe.
