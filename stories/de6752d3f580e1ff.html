2027: Fintiri announces Adamawa APC campaign council, names Marwa co-chair

2026-10-26

Adamawa Governor Ahmadu Fintiri has announced the composition of the All Progressives Congress (APC) Campaign Council for the 2027 general elections in the state. Fintiri will chair the council, with retired Brigadier General Buba Marwa serving as co-chairman.

Deputy Governor Kaletapwa Farauta has been named Deputy Chairman I, while Ahmed Galadima, the APC governorship candidate, will serve as Deputy Chairman II. Titus Solomon will be the council’s Director General, and Amb. Peter Kevin has been appointed Campaign Director.

The council includes party stalwarts, stakeholders, and youth and women leaders drawn from Adamawa’s 21 local government areas. Fintiri urged its members to work together to secure victory for the APC at all levels in the forthcoming elections. He said the council was designed to reflect the party’s commitment to inclusivity and grassroots mobilisation.
