CIS confab to rally capital mobilisation for infrastructure, manufacturing

2026-10-27

The Chartered Institute of Stockbrokers will hold its 30th annual conference in Lagos on November 4 and 5, bringing together economic policymakers, financial market regulators, institutional investors and industry leaders. The event will focus on how to direct capital towards productive investment and support economic growth.

Under the theme “From Capital Mobilised to Prosperity Realised: Financing Nigeria’s Next Era of Growth and Global Competitiveness,” the conference will consider how money raised through the capital market can support business expansion, job creation and competitiveness. CIS President Dr Fiona Ahimie said the discussion should look beyond the amount of capital raised to how effectively it is deployed to expand productive capacity and strengthen businesses. She also identified innovation, inclusion and efficiency as important to attracting domestic and international investment.

Conference Committee Chairman Saheed Bashir said the gathering is intended to bring together participants from across the investment value chain and develop practical recommendations for financing Nigeria’s growth ambitions. Planned discussions will examine the connections between capital market development, technology, regulatory effectiveness and the funding requirements of the real economy.

A major session, titled “Financing Productive Investments in the Real Economy: Spotlight on Infrastructure, Manufacturing, and SMEs,” will focus on expanding access to long-term finance for sectors considered important to development. It will explore how capital markets can complement bank lending by linking businesses and infrastructure projects with longer-term funding from institutional and other investors. Organisers say the conference aims to produce proposals that policymakers, regulators, investors and industry leaders can put into practice.
