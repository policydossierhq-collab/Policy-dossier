De la Fuente Wins Men’s Ballon d’Or Coach Of The Year

2026-10-26

Luis de la Fuente received the Ballon d’Or men’s coach of the year award at a ceremony in London on Monday, following Spain’s World Cup victory. He credited the team’s success to players who put collective goals first and said the honour recognised work carried out over many years.

De la Fuente, 65, built his career through Spain’s national coaching system rather than as a former star player. After a playing career and a less successful period in club coaching, he applied in 2013 for a youth-team position advertised by the Spanish Football Federation. He began with the Under-19s, and some players from that group later featured in teams he led to the Nations League, Euro 2024 and World Cup titles.

Spain’s route to the World Cup trophy included a goalless draw with tournament debutants Cape Verde in its opening match. The team gained momentum and beat France 2-0 in the semi-final. In a close final against Lionel Messi’s Argentina in New Jersey, Spain held firm against physical opposition before Ferran Torres scored the winning goal in a 1-0 victory.

De la Fuente said Spanish football’s coaching and youth development provide a model for building future success. The women’s coach of the year award also went to a Spaniard: Barcelona’s Pere Romeu, recognised after leading the club to the Spanish league, Spanish cup, Super Cup and Champions League titles in one season.
