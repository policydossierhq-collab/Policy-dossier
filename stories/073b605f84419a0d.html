FAAC disburses N2.4trn to FG, states, LGs in October

2026-11-19

The Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) distributed N2.441 trillion in October 2026 revenue among the federal, state and local governments. The allocation, agreed at a meeting in Abuja on Wednesday, comprised N1.688 trillion in distributable statutory revenue and N752.488 billion in Value Added Tax (VAT) revenue.

The committee reported that gross revenue of N3.811 trillion was available for the month. Deductions for collection costs totalled N128.797 billion, while transfers, refunds and savings amounted to N1.241 trillion. Gross statutory revenue was N2.999 trillion, down N97.323 billion from N3.096 trillion in September. Gross VAT revenue was N812.446 billion, a decrease of N4.426 billion from the previous month’s N816.872 billion.

Of the total distributable sum, the Federal Government received N856.065 billion and state governments received N809.909 billion. Local Government Councils were allocated N568.701 billion, while N206.384 billion was shared with benefiting states as derivation revenue, representing 13 per cent of mineral revenue.

Within the statutory revenue allocation, the Federal Government received N780.816 billion, states N396.040 billion and Local Government Councils N305.331 billion. The N206.384 billion derivation payment was also included in that distribution. From VAT, the Federal Government received N75.249 billion, state governments N413.868 billion and local councils N263.371 billion.

FAAC said excise duty and Common External Tariff levies rose marginally in October. Petroleum Profit Tax, Hydrocarbon Tax, petroleum royalties, import duty, Companies Income Tax, capital gains and stamp duties, VAT, mineral royalties, gas-flaring penalties and fees, and miscellaneous oil revenue all declined.
