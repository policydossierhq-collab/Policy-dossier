Ebola: Suspected index patient evacuated from Nigeria by air ambulance – Report

2026-10-26

A woman reportedly identified as Nigeria’s suspected Ebola index patient was evacuated from the country by air ambulance early Monday, according to a report by ARISE News. The report said the evacuation took place through the private aviation section of Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport in Abuja.

ARISE News said sources had indicated the woman, reportedly the wife of a Swiss diplomat in Nigeria, was expected to leave at about 6 a.m. Its crew at the airport reportedly saw ambulances and diplomatic vehicles arrive at the General Aviation Terminal. Airport officials then cordoned off the area, the report said.

The news organisation later obtained video footage showing a medical evacuation aircraft and its crew preparing to transport a passenger. ARISE News said the circumstances led it to suspect that the passenger was the reported Ebola index patient. It also cited a source who said the Federal Government would announce the evacuation after the aircraft had left Nigerian airspace.

The report did not include official confirmation from the Federal Government, the Nigeria Centre for Disease Control and Prevention, or airport authorities establishing the passenger’s identity, diagnosis or destination. Those details, and the circumstances of the reported evacuation, remain unconfirmed.

Source: Vanguard
