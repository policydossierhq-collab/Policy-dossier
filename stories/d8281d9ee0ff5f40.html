NGX plunges N3.28 trillion as Dangote Cement, Presco, UACN, HBM Nigeria, Tier 1 banks fall

2026-11-05

Nigeria’s equities market shed about N3.28 trillion on Thursday, November 5, 2026, as selling in heavyweight stocks drove the benchmark lower. The NGX All-Share Index fell 5,052.22 points, or 1.98%, to 249,710.59 from 254,762.81 in the previous session. Market capitalisation declined to N162.13 trillion from N165.42 trillion.

Dangote Cement and Presco each fell by the maximum 10%. UACN lost 7.85% and HBM Nigeria declined 7.71%. Selling in First HoldCo, Nigerian Exchange Group, Nigerian Breweries, Stanbic IBTC, GTCO and UBA added to the downward pressure. Among those large-cap stocks, Stanbic IBTC fell 0.85% to N162.50, GTCO slipped 0.81% to N122.00 and UBA eased 0.24% to N42.00.

Dangote Cement was the largest single drag on market value. Its N149 decline accounted for roughly N2.5 trillion, or about three-quarters of the day’s total loss. The stock closed at N1,341, down from N1,490 on Wednesday. Despite the fall, it remained about 33% above its October 19 close of N1,008, following a 5.50% drop that day which removed N1.47 trillion from the market.

Market breadth weakened, with 36 stocks declining against 20 advancing, and all six sector indices finished lower. Industrial stocks recorded the sharpest sector decline. The year-to-date market return moderated to 60.47% from 63.72%, while the month-to-date return turned negative at about 1.8%.

Trading increased as prices fell: investors exchanged 856.28 million shares worth N74.09 billion in 33,540 deals. The market’s decline more than erased the N307.4 billion added in the previous sessions, when buying in Transcorp Power and banking stocks lifted prices. Continued selling in large-cap shares could keep the market volatile, while bargain-hunting in oversold heavyweights may offer support.
