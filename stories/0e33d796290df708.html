Police detain officer over fatal shooting of young girl in Lagos

2026-11-07

A Lagos police officer has been disarmed and detained after a young girl was fatally shot and another child injured during a police operation in the Nosamu area of Ajegunle. The incident occurred on Thursday night, November 5, 2026, as officers responded to a violent clash over toll collection between two rival groups, known as the Nosamu Boys and the Alayabiagba Boys.

Police spokesperson SP Abimbola Adebisi said the factions were reportedly armed with cutlasses and other dangerous weapons. During the operation to restore order, Assistant Superintendent of Police Omotayo Olajide discharged his firearm, and a bullet struck the two girls, according to the statement issued on Saturday.

Both children were taken to Ajeromi General Hospital for emergency treatment. Omole Taiwo was later transferred to Lagos State University Teaching Hospital in Ikeja, where she was pronounced dead. The second child remains in hospital and is reported to be in stable condition.

The police command said the case has been transferred to the State Criminal Investigation Department for a thorough and impartial inquiry into the circumstances of the shooting. The investigation is intended to establish what happened and determine any appropriate legal or disciplinary measures. Commissioner of Police Tijani Fatai visited Taiwo’s family to offer condolences and said no officer found culpable would be shielded. The command expressed sorrow over the loss of life and urged anyone with relevant information to contact a nearby police station or its Public Relations Department.
