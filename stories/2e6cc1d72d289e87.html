2027: Turnout at Kaduna rally shows North’s support for Tinubu — Group

2026-10-25

A northern group said the large turnout at a rally in Kaduna showed the region’s confidence in President Bola Tinubu’s leadership ahead of 2027.

The group presented attendance at the rally as evidence of support for Tinubu. The statement links that support to the period ahead of 2027.

The claim is the group’s assessment of the rally turnout. The supplied information does not provide further details about the rally or the group.
