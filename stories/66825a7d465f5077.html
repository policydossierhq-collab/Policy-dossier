Currency outside banks rises 104% in 3 yrs — CBN

2026-11-03

Currency held outside Nigeria’s banking system rose 103.8 per cent over three years, reaching N4.923 trillion in September 2026 from N2.416 trillion in September 2023, according to Central Bank of Nigeria Money and Credit Statistics. The total was also 10.3 per cent higher than the N4.465 trillion recorded in September 2025.

The annual figures show currency outside banks increased by 66.2 per cent to N4.016 trillion in September 2024, then rose by 11.2 per cent to N4.465 trillion a year later. It climbed further to N4.923 trillion in September 2026. The continuing increase points to substantial demand for physical cash even as mobile banking, electronic transfers, point-of-sale terminals and other digital payment channels have expanded.

Demand deposits also grew over the period, rising 70.8 per cent to N39.124 trillion in September 2026 from N22.903 trillion in September 2023. They reached N31.842 trillion in September 2024, up 39 per cent from a year earlier, and N34.642 trillion in September 2025, an annual increase of 8.8 per cent. The latest figure represented a 12.9 per cent rise from September 2025. Demand deposits are funds in bank accounts generally accessible for payments and withdrawals on demand.

Economic and communications expert Clifford Egbomeade said the growth in both measures reflects the presence of formal bank balances alongside strong demand for cash in Nigeria’s payment system. He cautioned that the measures track different forms of monetary activity: demand deposits are account balances, while currency outside banks is cash held beyond the banking system. Their growth rates alone, he said, do not show whether higher cash holdings result from economic growth, inflation, informal transactions or changing payment preferences.
