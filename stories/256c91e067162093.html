Ebonyi Youths Protest Political Violence, Intimidation Ahead Of 2027 Elections

2026-11-06

Hundreds of young people from across Ebonyi State marched through Abakaliki, calling for an end to political violence, intimidation and harassment of opposition members ahead of the 2027 general elections. The group, Concerned Ebonyi Youth for Democracy, said participants came from all 13 local government areas to press for a political environment where people can take part without fear.

The demonstrators carried placards calling for peaceful political debate and protection of freedom of speech. A spokesperson said the group would not accept efforts to undermine citizens’ rights to express themselves and assemble peacefully.

The group also raised concern about reported physical violence, harassment and voter intimidation directed at opposition figures in parts of the state. It said political thuggery and threats could harm the democratic process, and argued that citizens should be able to participate regardless of party affiliation.

The protesters asked security agencies and state institutions to intervene and help ensure a fair environment for all political parties. A youth coordinator said opposition parties should be able to campaign, present their programmes and engage voters without threats. The group described the march as part of a wider effort by young people to demand accountability and support peaceful political participation as the 2027 elections approach.
