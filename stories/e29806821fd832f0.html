Atiku Verdict: APC wants opposition fighting – Ameh

2026-11-06

High Chief Peter Ameh, the National Democratic Congress (NDC) senatorial candidate for Kogi East, has urged opposition parties not to treat the court judgment affecting the African Democratic Congress (ADC) as a political victory. The ruling sacked the David Mark-led ADC National Working Committee and nullified Atiku Abubakar’s candidacy, according to the account of the decision provided by Ameh.

Speaking in Abuja on Thursday, Ameh warned that celebrating the outcome could deepen divisions among opposition groups. He argued that the All Progressives Congress (APC) would benefit if opposition parties weakened one another, irrespective of their political affiliations. He also cautioned that a ruling seen as favouring one faction could later be used against another.

Ameh described the judgment as a warning rather than a win for any side. He urged opposition parties to focus on pressing for free, fair and credible elections instead of becoming consumed by disputes over party leadership and candidates.

He also criticised the APC-led administration, accusing it of failing to provide good governance over the past 11 years. Ameh called on opposition groups to work together to end the party’s rule, saying the APC was responsible for the country’s governance problems.
