Political Signals: The Price Of Participation

Police said six people died in violence at a campaign event in Ughelli North, Delta State, on Monday. The preliminary figure, announced Tuesday, is higher than the four deaths first reported by Ovie Omo-Agege’s campaign organisation. The circumstances and responsibility remain under investigation. Authorities have imposed a daily curfew across Ughelli North from 6 p.m. to 6 a.m. The NDC has alleged official complicity, while the Delta State Government has rejected attempts to link Governor Sheriff Oborevwori to the violence, condemned the killings and promised accountability. Those claims remain distinct from the findings of the investigation.

The Federal High Court in Warri on Tuesday nullified Omo-Agege’s nomination as the NDC’s Delta Central senatorial candidate and directed the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) to remove his name. Reports attributed the ruling to failures involving the party’s membership register and nomination requirements. Responses from Omo-Agege and the NDC were not available in initial reports. The judgment and the campaign violence are separate matters; their timing does not establish a connection. Any appeal, interim order or subsequent INEC action could affect the candidacy’s status.

In Lagos, President Bola Tinubu met with Igbo stakeholders, who the presidency said pledged support. Tinubu offered assurances of inclusive governance, while traders’ representative Tony Nwakeze requested loans at five per cent interest to help businesses expand and move towards manufacturing. No operational loan scheme was announced. The Central Bank’s Monetary Policy Committee retained the benchmark interest rate at 23 per cent, underscoring the tension between economic stability and demands for more affordable business financing.

Opposition parties are due to hold a summit on November 30, after postponing it from November 23. Organisers say participating parties and candidates are expected to endorse a framework for cooperation, democratic renewal and reform; no common presidential candidate has been announced. INEC has also selected 17,676 polling units for a nationwide mock accreditation and results-upload exercise on November 28, scheduled to run from 8:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. The test will allow participants to assess the technology, while published findings and documented follow-up would help the public judge how problems are addressed. The Delta killings make campaign safety an immediate concern alongside candidate eligibility and election readiness.
