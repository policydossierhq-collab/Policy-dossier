‘Atiku majored in hygiene, cannot rule Nigeria’s modern economy’ — Shettima

Vice President Kashim Shettima criticised former vice president Atiku Abubakar’s proposal to restore fuel subsidy, arguing it would take Nigeria back towards poverty. Shettima made the remarks at the Nigerian Economic Summit in Abuja on Monday, accusing Atiku of supporting the policy for political convenience.

“One of the leading presidential candidates is advocating for the return of the subsidy scheme. He wants us to backslide into penury for political convenience,” Shettima said. He also challenged Atiku’s suitability to govern, referring to his training at the Kano School of Hygiene and saying he lacked the skill set to manage a modern economy.

Shettima further questioned Atiku’s knowledge of environmental sanitation, saying he did not think the former vice president could distinguish between flu and Ebola. He urged Nigerians to weigh political leaders’ proposals and qualifications before making decisions, warning those at the summit not to “play the ostrich.”

Atiku has advocated restoring the subsidy as part of his proposed approach to managing the economy. The issue remains a point of disagreement between the opposition and the administration of President Bola Tinubu, which removed the subsidy in May 2023. The government has defended the decision as necessary to reduce pressure on public finances, while the removal has been accompanied by higher fuel prices and increased living costs for many Nigerians.
