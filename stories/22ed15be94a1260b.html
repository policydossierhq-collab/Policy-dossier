Tinubu aware of food prices – Presidential aide

2026-10-27

President Bola Tinubu receives regular briefings about the prices of everyday food items, including eggs and bread, according to his Senior Special Assistant on Community Engagement for the North-Central region, Dr Abiodun Essiet. Speaking to State House correspondents at the Presidential Villa in Abuja on Tuesday, Essiet said community representatives and members of the Federal Executive Council communicate information about the pressures Nigerians face. She disputed the idea that journalists alone understand those challenges.

Essiet also described infrastructure as central to the administration’s development agenda, pointing to the Mokwa-Bida road in Niger State. She said residents had repeatedly called for the route to be addressed and that a crowd gathered when she visited a section of it. According to Essiet, the road was last awarded a contract 46 years ago. She added that heavy vehicles had been involved in accidents while attempting to navigate it, and said people had died on the road.

Essiet said Tinubu had directed that the road contract be awarded with a requirement for reinforced concrete construction rather than ordinary asphalt, which she said was intended to make the work durable. She was appointed to her North-Central community engagement role in 2024. Separately, Vice President Kashim Shettima said on October 19 that food prices would fall in the coming weeks as the government responds to agricultural challenges.
