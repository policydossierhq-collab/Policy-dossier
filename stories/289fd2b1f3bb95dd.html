NAFDAC Uncovers Expired Baby Diapers, Balms, Others In Lagos

2026-11-20

The National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC) says its investigation and enforcement officers found expired and unregistered products in a store at Idumota, Lagos State. The discovery was made during an operation in the area, the agency said in a post on its official X account on Friday.

The products included assorted brands of baby diapers, methylated balms, unregistered baby wipes, disposable pants and other medical products. NAFDAC said some diapers and balms were past their expiry dates, with some expired since 2022. The agency did not provide further details about the quantities or brands found.

NAFDAC said all the offending products had been removed from the store. Investigations are ongoing to establish the circumstances of their storage and how they were intended to be handled. The agency said its enforcement work is aimed at preventing expired and unregistered regulated products from reaching consumers.

The Lagos discovery followed a separate interception announced by NAFDAC at Mallam Aminu Kano International Airport. The agency said 17 cartons of suspected unregistered beverages and skin-lightening and anti-ageing injections were found concealed in a consignment declared as personal effects. The shipment lacked required import documents and was held in the custody of the Nigeria Customs Service while the importer was invited for questioning. NAFDAC cautioned that the quality and authenticity of those products had not been established.
