DMO raises N5.06 billion from November savings bonds, down 17.4%

2026-11-12

The Debt Management Office (DMO) allotted N5.06 billion through its November 2026 Federal Government of Nigeria Savings Bond offer, down 17.4% from the N6.12 billion allotted in October. The November total was about N1.06 billion lower, and the number of subscriptions received also declined.

The allotment comprised N746.01 million in the two-year bond and N4.31 billion in the three-year instrument. The offer was open from November 2 to 6 and settled on November 11. Annual interest rates were 13.25% for the two-year bond and 14.25% for the three-year bond.

The lower November allotment came despite an increase in the rates offered on both tenors. The available results do not specify how many subscriptions were received or give a breakdown of the change in participation. They do show that less was allocated to investors through the retail bond offer than in October.

FGN Savings Bonds are among the government’s domestic borrowing instruments. The DMO also manages larger FGN bond issuances; in June 2026, it announced an approximately N4 trillion auction plan for the third quarter. The November results follow a period of higher allotments in the retail government securities market during the first nine months of 2026.
