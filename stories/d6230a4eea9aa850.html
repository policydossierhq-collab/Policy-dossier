Three killed, houses burnt in Cross River communal clash

2026-11-28

Three people have died and several others were injured in a communal clash between factions from Ijiman and Akamkpa wards in Ago-I Ekpo, Yakurr Local Government Area of Cross River State. Two houses were burnt, while other buildings and property were destroyed or damaged, the state police command said.

The clash began at about 4pm on Friday, November 27, 2026, during a festival celebration, following a disagreement between two young men from the opposing wards, according to police. The command said supporters from both sides joined the confrontation, which escalated into violence. Police spokesperson ASP Eitokpah Sunday Akata announced the details in a statement on Saturday.

Police Commissioner Ajo Geoffrey Ordue directed officers from Nko Division and the Violent Crime Response Unit of the Ugep Area Command to the community after receiving a report of the clash. Their intervention brought the situation under control, the command said, while community leaders and representatives of the factions were engaged to help prevent further hostilities. The bodies of the dead were taken to a hospital in Ugep for preservation, and the injured are receiving treatment at various health facilities.

Police said officers remained in the community to maintain order and guard against reprisal attacks. Investigations are continuing to establish the circumstances of the violence and identify anyone responsible. The commissioner urged residents, especially young people and community leaders, to exercise restraint and resolve disputes through dialogue. He also cautioned against reprisals and further damage to property. The command called on residents to remain calm, cooperate with investigators and report credible information about suspicious activity or threats to public peace.
