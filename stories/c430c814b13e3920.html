Death toll in Taraba terror attack rises to 40

2026-10-25

The reported death toll from a terror attack in Taraba has risen to 40, according to Premium Times. The publication had earlier reported that state police confirmed 10 local people were killed.

The latest figure is four times the number initially confirmed by police. The source material does not provide further details about when the attack occurred, where in Taraba it took place or how the higher toll was established.

The increase marks a significant change in the reported scale of the attack. The available information does not identify those responsible or give details about any official response.

Source: Premium Times
