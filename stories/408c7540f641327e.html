Mike Adenuga: Blurring the line between commerce and compassion, By Tayo Williams

2026-10-31

Mike Adenuga’s philanthropy has included direct support for security and public health, alongside programmes run through the Mike Adenuga Foundation. Earlier this year, he donated ₦1 billion through Globacom to the Lagos State Security Trust Fund to support security infrastructure. In 2020, during the COVID-19 pandemic, he gave ₦1 billion to support federal response efforts, particularly the Private Sector Coalition against COVID-19, and a further ₦500 million to the Lagos State Government. He also indicated that his business group was willing to help supply protective equipment and other essential items.

The foundation, established to support social and economic development in Nigeria and other African countries, focuses on entrepreneurship, education, health and rural development. Its work aims to provide knowledge, skills, resources and infrastructure, and also addresses the needs of vulnerable people and internally displaced persons, as well as disaster relief and health crises.

Adenuga’s support for the entertainment industry has largely been channelled through Globacom. Since the company began operating in 2003, it has used Nigerian entertainers as brand ambassadors and provided sponsorship and other financial backing. The company also made per-second billing available when it entered the market, a change the supplied account describes as broadening access to mobile telephony. Globacom later introduced services including 2.5G, multimedia messaging and international roaming.

Adenuga’s wider business interests include telecommunications, banking, oil and gas, real estate and construction. He founded Devcom Bank and Equitorial Trust Bank, which were later consolidated; Equitorial Trust Bank subsequently merged with Sterling Bank. He also holds interests in Julius Berger through Goldstones Estates and in Conoil through Conpetro. His business activities and philanthropic work have earned him national and international honours, including Nigeria’s Grand Commander of the Order of the Niger.
