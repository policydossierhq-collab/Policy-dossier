RCIM share disposal records good progress as investor demand remains robust

2026-10-29

First HoldCo Plc and RC Investment Management Limited (RCIM) say they have made significant progress in transferring a large block of FirstHoldCo shares held by RCIM as an independent Bridge Holder. The disposal is an orderly transfer of existing shares through the Nigerian Exchange (NGX), rather than a new share issue.

RCIM acquired approximately 10.43 billion FirstHoldCo shares on the NGX in July 2025 under a Central Bank of Nigeria-approved shareholder divestment arrangement. About 5.2 billion shares have since been crossed to subscribers. Further transfers are expected once the required regulatory approvals are secured and the remaining execution processes are completed.

The companies said the large trading volumes in FirstHoldCo shares reflect, to a substantial extent, the transfer of RCIM’s holding to investors. They described subscriptions and the absorption of the sizeable block as evidence of continued investor confidence, citing the group’s financial performance, fundamentals and long-term growth prospects.

The statement also pointed to shareholder returns. On a total shareholder return basis, First HoldCo ranks first among the FUGAZ banking groups, with returns of more than 200% from the beginning of January 2026 to date, it said. The disposal is intended to complete the divestment programme in an orderly manner and broaden FirstHoldCo’s investor base.
