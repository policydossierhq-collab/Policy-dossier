Hakimi bags hat-trick as Paris Saint-Germain rip Le Havre to pieces

2026-10-30

Achraf Hakimi scored three times as Paris Saint-Germain came from behind to beat Le Havre 5-1 on Friday. The result lifted the champions to 20 points after nine Ligue 1 matches and opened a four-point lead over nearest rivals Rennes, who lost 5-2 at Nice.

Le Havre, bottom of the table, took the lead in the 18th minute when Josh Maja struck against the run of play. PSG responded quickly: Mika Godts ran onto a through-ball from Hakimi to equalise in the 26th minute, and Hakimi put the visitors ahead two minutes later with a low shot inside the near post.

Hakimi extended PSG’s advantage with a free-kick that curled past goalkeeper Arnaud Bodard. After the hour, Senny Mayulu made it 4-1, finishing from distance on a break while Le Havre’s goalkeeper was well away from his line. Hakimi completed his hat-trick in the 69th minute, threading a shot through a crowded area. Coach Luis Enrique substituted him shortly after.

Luis Enrique rested Desire Doue, Vitinha and Joao Neves, while Ousmane Dembele and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia were unavailable through injury. PSG’s position at the top could still change: Lille could move within two points with a win over Lens later on Friday, and Monaco had the same opportunity ahead of their Sunday match at Paris FC.
