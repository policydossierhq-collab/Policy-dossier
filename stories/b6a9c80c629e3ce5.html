Rema Releases Seven-Track EP ‘E-Mortal Boy’, Features Wizkid On First Collaboration

2026-11-20

Nigerian singer Rema has released ‘E-Mortal Boy’, a seven-track, 22-minute EP and his first extended play since ‘Ravage’ in 2023. Also styled as EMB, the project was released on Friday through Mavin Global Holdings and Jonzing World Entertainment, with distribution involving Interscope/Universal Music Group. It combines five new songs with two previously released singles, ‘Oh No’ and ‘TEA’.

The opening track, ‘Feel Alright’, features Wizkid and marks the first official musical collaboration between the two artists. The song focuses on romantic attraction and a lover’s desires. Thisizlondon and AoD produced the track, and both performers are credited as songwriters. The pairing also marks Rema’s first official track with any of the Nigerian artists commonly grouped as the “Big Three”: Wizkid, Davido and Burna Boy.

The EP’s other tracks bring together a range of collaborators and influences. ‘TANZANIA’ features American producer BNYX and draws on Bollywood-inspired sounds, while ‘SUGA’, with French-Congolese singer Theodora, incorporates grunge influences. The closing track, ‘Bang Bang’, features Nigerian-American singer Kah-Lo and producer Thisizlondon. The full tracklist is ‘Feel Alright’, ‘Oh No’, ‘Velvet’, ‘TANZANIA’, ‘TEA’, ‘SUGA’ and ‘Bang Bang’.

Thisizlondon, a longtime Rema collaborator who has worked on songs including ‘Calm Down’ and ‘Soundgasm’, also has production credits across the project. The release follows Rema’s 2024 second album, ‘HEIS’, which received a Grammy nomination. With ‘E-Mortal Boy’, he continues to explore different musical influences while assembling a varied group of collaborators; the Wizkid pairing is the EP’s most prominent new collaboration.
