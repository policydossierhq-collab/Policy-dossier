[FULL LIST] Umejiaku Wins Golden Ball At U-17 Women’s World Cup

2026-11-07

Nigeria forward Oluebube Umejiaku has been named the adidas Golden Ball winner at the 2026 FIFA U-17 Women’s World Cup in Morocco. She received the award after helping the Flamingos win their first title, defeating Japan 2-1 in Saturday’s final in Rabat. Umejiaku scored Nigeria’s second goal and finished the tournament with four goals.

DPR Korea’s Yu Jong-hyang won the Silver Ball, and Japan’s Fuki Ito took the Bronze Ball. Yu also received the Silver Boot. DPR Korea’s Ri Ui-gyong was awarded the Bronze Boot.

The Golden Boot went to United States forward Maddie DiMaria, the tournament’s top scorer with 12 goals. DPR Korea goalkeeper Kim Son-gyong won the Golden Glove, while Japan received the FIFA Fair Play Trophy.
