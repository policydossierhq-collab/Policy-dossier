Police ‌‌‍‌‍⁠⁠‍‍⁠‍‌‌‍‍‌detain man for alleged unlawful possession of firearm

2026-11-24

Police in Enugu State say Odo Ejiofor, 28, is in custody over the alleged unlawful possession of a firearm. In a statement issued in Enugu on Monday, police spokesperson Daniel Ndukwe said operatives recovered firearms during operations at suspected criminal hideouts across the state on 21 November. The statement gave details of three operations and said investigations were continuing.

At about 7 a.m., the Violent Crime Response Unit’s Anti-Cultism Section raided a suspected hideout at Old Artisan, Enugu, after receiving information. Police said suspects fled and abandoned a pump-action gun, which officers recovered. At about 3:30 p.m., Udenu Area Command operatives raided a suspected hideout at Ugbelle-Ohullor, Obollo Afor, in Udenu Local Government Area. They arrested Ejiofor and recovered a locally made pistol, according to the statement.

At about 6:05 p.m., members of the Violent Crime Response Unit raided a hideout at Abakpa-Nike, Enugu, acting on intelligence, and recovered a pistol. The suspects fled when officers arrived. Police said the location was reportedly used for the sale and consumption of illicit drugs and other criminal activities.

Ndukwe said police were working to identify and arrest the suspects who escaped. Ejiofor is expected to be arraigned after investigations are concluded. Commissioner of Police Fidelis Ogarabe reiterated the command’s commitment to intelligence gathering and targeted operations, and urged residents to provide credible, timely information, which he said would be treated confidentially.
