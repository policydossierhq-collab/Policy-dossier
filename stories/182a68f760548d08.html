Voters’ register: INEC clarifies ward display, website delay

2026-10-26

Nigeria’s Independent National Electoral Commission says the preliminary voters’ register has been made available at ward level but has not been uploaded to its official website. Mohammed Haruna, the commission’s national commissioner and chairman of its Information and Voter Education Committee, said the register had been published locally, but not online.

The clarification comes amid concern about access to the register during its scheduled display period of October 9 to 15, 2026. Former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar called on INEC to make the detailed register available online, saying voters need to check their information and identify omissions or questionable entries ahead of the 2027 general election.

Atiku said INEC had announced more than 103 million registered voters, but that people seeking to inspect names on its portal encountered an empty page. He argued that publishing a total number of voters was not a substitute for allowing individuals to verify their own registration details.

Section 19(1) of the Electoral Act 2026 requires the commission to make copies of the register available for public scrutiny at registration areas and on its official website, or another website established for that purpose, for seven days. The provision says the display period must be appointed no later than 90 days before a general election. The display is intended to let voters confirm their details and enable the public to flag potential omissions or questionable entries before the register is finalised.
