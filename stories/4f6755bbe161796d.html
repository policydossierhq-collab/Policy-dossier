Ebola: Patient Arrives Switzerland, Nigeria Continues Contact Tracing, Containment — Pate

2026-10-27

Nigeria’s Ebola index patient and her spouse have arrived safely in Switzerland, Health and Social Welfare Minister Ali Pate said. The patient is receiving treatment at Geneva University Hospitals, while her husband has been placed in isolation at University Hospital Zurich. Nigerian authorities continue to monitor contacts, investigate alerts and carry out other containment measures, Pate said in an update on Monday evening.

Pate said Switzerland had thanked Nigerian health professionals for their care and recognised the cooperation between the two countries and the World Health Organisation that enabled the evacuation. He also acknowledged the work of health teams and agencies in both countries, including the Nigeria Centre for Disease Control and Prevention, the Swiss Federal Office of Public Health and the WHO. Africa CDC and WHO AFRO are supporting Nigeria’s continuing response.

Earlier on Monday, Pate said the patient had left Abuja with his approval. He described her as clinically much improved, stable, conscious and able to communicate, with laboratory results approaching baseline levels. He cautioned that improvement did not necessarily mean she was no longer infectious, and said strict public health protocols were followed during the evacuation.

Nigeria confirmed its first imported Ebola virus disease case in the Federal Capital Territory on October 20, after the patient had travelled from Uganda. The infection was identified as the Bundibugyo strain. Authorities activated contact tracing, surveillance and monitoring after confirmation. Initial government information put the number of identified contacts at 44; a later WHO update said 52 people were being monitored in Nigeria, in addition to the patient’s husband. Pate said further updates would come through the NCDC and the Federal Ministry of Health and Social Welfare.
