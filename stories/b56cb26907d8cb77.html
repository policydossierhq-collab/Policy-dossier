Mbappe’s Brace Helps Real Madrid Beat Barcelona In La Liga Clasico

2026-10-25

Kylian Mbappe scored twice as Real Madrid defeated Barcelona 2-1 at Camp Nou on Sunday, handing the La Liga leaders their first domestic loss of the campaign. The result cut Madrid’s gap to Barcelona to three points, although Madrid remain third behind Barcelona and Atletico Madrid. Raphinha scored Barcelona’s only goal, equalising after Mbappe had put the visitors ahead before half-time.

The opening half was tight, with Barcelona defender Pau Cubarsi twice stopping Mbappe and Andriy Lunin making a save from Anthony Gordon. Madrid took the lead when Gerard Martin’s attempted clearance struck Mbappe and deflected into the net. Barcelona had a chance to respond before the break, but Lamine Yamal fired wide. Xavi Espart also had to leave the field injured before the half-hour mark, with Alejandro Balde coming on.

Barcelona improved after the interval and drew level just after the hour. Pedri set up Raphinha, who shot through Jude Bellingham’s legs; Lunin could not keep the effort out. The hosts then pressed for a second, but Lunin denied Martin and Yamal missed the target, while Dean Huijsen blocked a shot from substitute Frenkie de Jong.

Madrid struck late after substitute Gabriel Jesus lost possession. Eduardo Camavinga got past Cubarsi and squared the ball for Mbappe to score into an unguarded net, with Barcelona goalkeeper Joan Garcia out of position. It was Madrid’s fifth straight win across all competitions, while Barcelona suffered a second consecutive defeat following their Champions League loss to Paris Saint-Germain. Midfielder Pedri said Barcelona had not played well, particularly in the first half, but noted that the league season was long.
