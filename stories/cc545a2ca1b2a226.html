LASCOPA takes action against Ikeja Electric, resolves 528 consumer complaints

2026-10-30

The Lagos State Consumer Protection Agency says it resolved 528 of 556 complaints against Ikeja Electric Distribution Company between January and September 2026. The agency’s latest update puts the resolution rate at about 95%, with 28 complaints still ongoing. It also says its interventions secured credit adjustments and refunds totalling N26,243,758.75 for affected consumers.

LASCOPA General Manager and Chief Executive Officer Afolabi Solebo said the agency was committed to fairness, accountability, transparency and improved service delivery in the electricity distribution sector. The agency said its mediation work is intended to help consumers obtain redress and encourage electricity providers to respond to grievances.

Yusuf Musiliu, head of the agency’s Complaints and Mediation Department, said LASCOPA receives and analyses complaints submitted through online and offline channels. Its monthly stakeholders’ parley with Ikeja Electric gives company representatives and affected consumers a regular forum to discuss unresolved cases and seek mutually acceptable agreements. The meetings take place on the last Thursday of each month, according to the agency.

LASCOPA advised consumers to use established complaint procedures rather than resort to self-help. It urged residents raising concerns about estimated bills, excessive charges, overbilling, electricity supply or other service practices to submit supporting evidence, such as bills and payment receipts, to assist investigations and speed up resolution. The agency said its consumer-protection work is carried out under the Lagos State Consumer Protection Law 2025, as amended, and encouraged residents to report grievances promptly.
