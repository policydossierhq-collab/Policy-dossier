Ballon d’Or 2026: Full top 10 ranking as Harry Kane claims award

2026-10-26

Harry Kane has won the 2026 Ballon d’Or, finishing first in a ranking that included some of football’s leading players. The Bayern Munich striker claimed the individual award ahead of Kylian Mbappé and Barcelona’s Lamine Yamal, who placed second and third respectively.

Manchester City midfielder Rodri took fourth place, followed by Lionel Messi in fifth. Paris Saint-Germain forward Khvicha Kvaratskhelia ranked sixth, with his teammate Ousmane Dembélé in seventh.

Erling Haaland, a striker for Manchester City, finished eighth. Bayern Munich winger Michael Olise was ninth, and Paris Saint-Germain midfielder Fabián Ruiz completed the top 10 in tenth place.

Kane’s win puts him ahead of a field that includes established award winners and players from the current generation. The final ranking placed contenders from Bayern Munich, Manchester City, Paris Saint-Germain and Barcelona among the leading positions.
