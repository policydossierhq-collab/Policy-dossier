2027: Ebonyi PDP inaugurates campaign council

2026-11-23

The Peoples Democratic Party (PDP) in Ebonyi State has inaugurated a campaign council ahead of the 2027 general elections. The party said the council includes political leaders, grassroots mobilisers, women and young people from the state’s 13 local government areas and 171 political wards.

According to a statement issued in Abakaliki and signed by state spokesperson Darlington Onwe, the council will coordinate campaign activities across Ebonyi, strengthen voter engagement and mobilise support for the party’s candidates in elective positions. The party said its work would also advance the political ambition of its governorship candidate, Ifeanyi-Chukwuma Odii.

Nwanne Ominyi was named director-general of the campaign, with Paul Okorie appointed deputy director-general for operations. Other named officials include Chigozie Omege, deputy director-general for Ebonyi North; Justus Ikechukwu-Okorie, deputy director-general for Ebonyi South; and Festus Okoha, deputy director-general for Ebonyi Central. Mudi Erenede was appointed director of legal services.

Ikechukwu Nwobo will serve as campaign spokesperson, while Okorie C. Okorie was named director of special duties and protocol. The campaign slogan is “Anyi G’Emeya,” which the statement translates as “Yes, We Can.” The party called on appointees, supporters and stakeholders to show unity, discipline and commitment, and to work together to rebuild and reposition the state.
