Senegal to know fate of AFCON title appeal Tuesday – CAS

2026-11-23

The Court of Arbitration for Sport (CAS) says it will deliver its decision on Tuesday on Senegal’s appeal against the removal of its Africa Cup of Nations title. In a statement issued Monday, the court said the parties had been told the ruling would be released during the afternoon of November 24, 2026, Greenwich Mean Time. CAS did not announce what the outcome would be.

The appeal concerns the final played in Rabat on January 18. Senegal initially won 1-0 against Morocco after Pape Gueye scored in extra time. But the match was disrupted late in normal time, when Senegal players left the pitch to protest a penalty awarded to Morocco while the score was still 0-0.

The players later returned, with the match delayed for almost 20 minutes. Some Senegalese supporters also attempted to enter the pitch. In March, the Confederation of African Football overturned the result, recording a 3-0 victory for Morocco and awarding the country the title.

The Senegalese Football Federation condemned that decision as the “most blatant administrative robbery in the history of our sport”. Senegal’s team also paraded the trophy before supporters in Paris at the end of March. CAS heard the appeal behind closed doors at its Lausanne headquarters on October 8. Tuesday’s ruling will determine whether CAF’s decision to award Morocco the title stands or is overturned.
