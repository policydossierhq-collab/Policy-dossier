FULL LIST: NDC names Peter Obi, Henry Dickson, others as campaign council members

2026-10-25

The Nigeria Democratic Congress has formed a National Campaign Council for the 2027 general elections, naming its National Leader, Senator Henry Dickson, as chairman and presidential candidate Peter Obi as co-chairman. The party announced the appointments in a statement posted on its X account on Sunday, saying the council would coordinate campaign activity nationwide and mobilise support for its vision of a “new, just, and prosperous Nigeria.”

The party’s National Chairman and vice-presidential candidate, Senator Cleopas Zuwoghe, was named vice-chairman, with Senator Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso as co-vice-chairman. National Secretary Ikenna Enekweizu is among the council’s leadership, while Ibrahim Abdulkarim was named council secretary and director-general of the presidential campaign. Other listed council members include Babachir Lawal, Ovie Omo-Agege, Victor Umeh, Buba Galadima and Dudu Manuga.

The announcement also sets out a National Campaign Advisory Council, chaired by Lawal, with Zuwoghe as co-chairman and former governor Sam Egwu as vice-chairman. Its membership includes state representatives across the country, among them Pat Utomi, Oseloka Obaze, Onyema Ugochukwu and Achike Udenwa. The party also named campaign directorates covering areas such as finance, communications, logistics, policy, legal affairs, security, election monitoring, youth and women’s mobilisation, and diaspora engagement.

State campaign coordinators were appointed across the states and the Federal Capital Territory. The list includes Senator Nkechi Nwogu for Abia, Bamanga Tukur Jr. for Adamawa, Ken Pela for Delta, Ife Salako for Lagos and Stephen Knabayi for the FCT. The entries for Nasarawa and Niger identify the coordinators only as senatorial candidates, without giving names.

The NDC said the council would provide strategic leadership and drive a “people-centred and issue-based campaign.” It urged party members, supporters and Nigerians to remain united and focused ahead of the polls, adding that “a new and working Nigeria is possible.”
