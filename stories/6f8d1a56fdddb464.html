Police React To Viral Video On Alleged Officers Brutality In Osun

2026-11-05

The Assistant Inspector-General of Police in charge of Zone 11, Bassey Ewah, has ordered an investigation after a video circulated on social media showing officers allegedly assaulting a suspect in Ede, Osun State. The incident reportedly took place on Wednesday, according to a statement issued on Thursday by the zone’s public relations officer, Ojedele Tunji.

Ewah said he viewed the footage with serious concern and stressed that the Nigeria Police Force does not condone brutality, intimidation or abuse of power. He said the officers involved had been identified and that steps had been initiated to investigate the incident thoroughly.

The AIG said police have the authority to enforce the law and maintain public order, but must carry out those duties professionally and with restraint. He also emphasised that officers are required to respect the fundamental rights and dignity of every person. Any officer found to have breached regulations or engaged in misconduct would face appropriate disciplinary measures under applicable laws and police regulations, the statement said.

Ewah urged the public to remain calm and avoid reaching conclusions before the investigation is complete. He said Zone 11, which serves Osun and Oyo states, remained committed to accountability, transparency and the protection of human rights, and that no officer was above the law.
