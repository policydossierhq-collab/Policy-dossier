Father, son jailed for smuggling £500,000 cannabis into UK

2026-11-25

A father and his 20-year-old son have been jailed for importing cannabis worth about £500,000 into the United Kingdom from Thailand. Alec Peter Chadwick, 39, and Riley Peter Emerson-Chadwick were arrested by Border Force officers at Heathrow Airport in July after arriving from Bangkok, the National Crime Agency (NCA) said.

Officers found a combined 50.25kg of cannabis in their luggage. Emerson-Chadwick had 33.5kg in his suitcase and Chadwick had 16.75kg. Both pleaded guilty to importing Class B drugs at Wimbledon Magistrates’ Court later that day.

At Isleworth Crown Court, Chadwick was sentenced to two years and eight months in prison, while his son received a two-year jail term. The NCA said organised crime groups often use social media to recruit couriers, offering free holidays and spending money in return for carrying drugs.

The agency reported that 142 cannabis smugglers were arrested at UK airports in 2023, followed by 801 in 2024 and 976 in 2025. It said 600 air-passenger drug couriers had been arrested at UK airports during the first six months of 2026, putting the year on course to exceed the previous total.

NCA senior investigating officer Peter Jones warned people against accepting offers from criminal networks, saying there was no such thing as a free holiday. He said couriers risked prison, criminal convictions and harm to their future career and travel prospects. Jones also warned that proceeds from cannabis smuggling can fund the trafficking of other drugs and contribute to wider criminal activity.
