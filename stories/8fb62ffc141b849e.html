Hotel accommodation: ‘This is not democracy, APC should allow parties to campaign without hindrance’ — NDC

2026-11-05

The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) has accused the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC) of trying to limit opposition activity, alleging that hotels in Abuja have denied it access to venues for meetings and events. In a statement posted on X on Thursday, the party urged the APC to allow political parties to campaign without hindrance ahead of the 2027 general election.

The NDC said it had been turned away by several hotels despite meeting their requirements for using the facilities. It alleged that one hotel, whose name it did not disclose, cancelled venue agreements twice, including arrangements for the party’s second National Executive Committee meeting in June 2026. The party said the hotel had also recently rejected another request, citing instructions from an undisclosed authority.

The party separately accused another hotel of refusing to provide a venue for a meeting even though it had complied with the hotel’s conditions. It also said the unveiling of its National Campaign Council was nearly disrupted when the host venue asked it to collect a refund and said it would no longer provide the facilities. According to the NDC, it had already paid and insisted on using the venue, after which the hotel honoured the arrangement.

The NDC described the alleged incidents as undemocratic and said they risked frustrating opposition activities and restricting political participation. It called on Nigerians and supporters of democracy to condemn what it characterised as anti-democratic actions by the hotels. The statement was signed by the party’s National Publicity Secretary, Osa Director, who reaffirmed the NDC’s commitment to serving Nigerians.
