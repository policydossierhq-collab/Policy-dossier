Tinubu defends borrowing plans, says investments ‘ll expand opportunities for future generations

2026-10-29

President Bola Tinubu has responded to criticism, particularly from opposition leaders, over new loans Nigeria has accessed from international institutions and banks.

In an essay titled “From reforms to delivery: Our infrastructure investment and growth strategy,” Tinubu defended the borrowing plans, saying the investments would expand opportunities for future generations.

The account provided no details about the loans’ amounts, terms or the specific projects they are intended to support.
