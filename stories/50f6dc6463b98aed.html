ADP crisis deepens as spokesman disputes Sani’s suspension

2026-11-08

The leadership dispute in the Action Democratic Party (ADP) has intensified after National Publicity Secretary Sani Dawop challenged the reported interim suspension of National Chairman Yabagi Yusuf Sani. National Secretary Victor Fingesi had announced the suspension over allegations of forged party documents and unauthorised interference with the Independent National Electoral Commission’s candidate nomination portal. Fingesi said the decision was approved by a majority of National Working Committee (NWC) members and communicated to INEC’s chairman. Sani declined to comment when contacted.

Dawop said the party’s leadership rejected that account and maintained that no constitutionally valid decision had been made to suspend or remove Sani. He said Sani remained the duly elected chairman following the party’s National Convention on May 29 and 30, 2026. According to Dawop, the NWC’s emergency meeting on November 4 concerned disciplinary issues involving Fingesi, including alleged insubordination and failure to follow lawful party directives.

Dawop said the meeting considered Fingesi’s responses to earlier queries and appeals for him to retract disputed statements and apologise. He said National Special Duties Secretary and Acting Deputy National Secretary Fatima Sani Muhammed moved a motion under Article 23.2(e) of the ADP constitution to suspend Fingesi from the functions of National Secretary, begin a disciplinary investigation and refer the matter to the National Executive Committee for ratification. Dawop said the motion was seconded and passed at a quorate meeting, with Fingesi and National Youth Leader Chinazam Ike among those present. He described the measure as interim, not a final finding of guilt.

Dawop said allegations against Sani, including claims of forgery, financial impropriety and interference with INEC’s portal, required verifiable evidence, investigation and a fair hearing. He called for independent inquiries into suspected unauthorised activity on the portal and preservation of electronic records, while saying those concerns did not establish wrongdoing by Sani or anyone else. He also said any financial audit should follow the party’s constitutional and financial procedures.

The spokesman warned against using party communications or letterhead to misrepresent decisions, saying the ADP could take constitutional or legal steps over communications that did so. He urged members, candidates and supporters to remain calm and focus on preparations for the 2027 general elections. Dawop’s account is that the November 4 decision concerned Fingesi, not Sani.
