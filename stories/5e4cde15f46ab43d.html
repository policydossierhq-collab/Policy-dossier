Gov Otti offers orphaned debate champion varsity scholarship

2026-10-25

Governor Otti has offered a university scholarship to an orphaned debate champion, according to the report. The source material does not identify the student or provide details about the scholarship.

Otti also awarded ₦1 million to Onyinyechi Okonkwo of Ovom Girls Secondary School, who won a recent national essay competition.

The report provides no further information about the debate competition or the terms of the scholarship.

Source: Vanguard
