‘Immigrants from shithouse country, not welcome,’ Trump blasts Norway over Nobel Peace Prize

2026-10-26

US President Donald Trump attacked Norway during a campaign rally in Port Huron, Michigan, saying the country had become a “shithouse country.” He also said he did not want immigrants from Norway because he had not received the Nobel Peace Prize.

Trump said he would welcome immigrants from some countries but not Norway, linking his remarks to the Nobel Committee’s decision. The rally took place ahead of the US midterm elections on November 3. There was no official indication that the comments represented a change in US policy toward Norwegian immigrants.

Trump has openly sought the Peace Prize since returning to the White House in January 2025. This year’s award went to Navi Pillay, a South African judge and former UN human rights chief. Trump has described the committee’s choice as “a disgraceful and embarrassing decision” and argued that the prize should have gone to him.

Norwegian Foreign Minister Espen Barth Eide responded on Monday, saying the committee operates independently. “He knows very well that the Nobel Committee is independent,” Eide told Norwegian broadcaster NRK, adding that this remained the case.

Trump faced criticism in 2018, during his first term, after using similar vulgar language about Haiti and some African countries.
