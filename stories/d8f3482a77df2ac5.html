Russia’s Ambassador to US, Alexander Darchiev, dies at 66

2026-11-12

Russia’s Foreign Ministry has announced the death of Alexander Darchiev, the country’s ambassador to the United States. He was 66. The ministry gave no details about the circumstances of his death, describing him in a tribute as an outstanding diplomat, an experienced leader and a kind colleague.

Darchiev was appointed to the Washington post in March 2025, shortly after Donald Trump returned to power. Before that, he served as Russia’s ambassador to Canada from 2021 to 2024.

A specialist in relations between Russia and the United States, Darchiev led the Foreign Ministry’s North America Department from 2014 to 2020. His appointment to Washington came as relations between Russia and Western countries were strained over Moscow’s military offensive in Ukraine.

After Russia’s 2022 invasion of Ukraine, Darchiev described the military action as a necessary measure. The Foreign Ministry’s announcement did not provide further information about his death.
