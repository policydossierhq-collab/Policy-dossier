‎2027: Delta CP warns officers against partisanship, extortion

2026-11-26

Delta State Commissioner of Police Samuel Erale has instructed senior officers to remain politically neutral ahead of the 2027 general elections and warned them against colluding with political parties or candidates. He issued the directive at a Thursday conference, following guidance from Inspector-General of Police Olatunji Disu. Erale said people should receive equal police protection regardless of political affiliation and urged officers to safeguard the public and the electoral process.

He told Area Commanders and Divisional Police Officers to work with campaign organisers on safety arrangements. If a planned venue cannot be policed effectively, he said, organisers should move the event to a more secure location where adequate protection can be provided.

The commissioner also called on officers to build stronger ties with the communities they serve. He urged them to maintain contact with residents and community leaders and use local intelligence to identify emerging security concerns. Amid a rising number of complaints about police conduct, Erale reaffirmed the command’s zero tolerance for extortion and corruption. He directed senior officers to improve supervision, respond promptly to complaints and hold personnel accountable, while mentoring junior officers.

The instructions come amid reports of violence connected to political campaigning in Delta. Six people were reported killed and several others injured at a campaign event in Uduere, in Ughelli North. A day earlier, the campaign organisation of former Deputy President of the Senate Ovie Omo-Agege alleged that gunmen attacked his entourage during a campaign tour, killing four people and critically injuring four others. The police confirmed that the state government had imposed a dusk-to-dawn curfew across Ughelli North, from 6 p.m. to 6 a.m. daily, until further notice. The Nigeria Democratic Congress, the Nigerian Bar Association and the Pan Niger Delta Forum have called for an investigation.
