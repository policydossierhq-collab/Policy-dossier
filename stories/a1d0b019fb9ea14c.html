Binance says has covered 90% of $6.3 billion hack losses

2026-10-31

Binance said Saturday it had used company funds to buy tokens and replace about 90 percent of the assets stolen in a major hack. The exchange said it remained committed to covering the full financial impact of the incident and had set aside more than $6.34 billion for that purpose.

The remaining 10 percent of tokens may take longer to replace, Binance said, because they are less liquid and may be harder to source in the quantities required. The company described the incident as a complex attack involving several points of breach, according to an update issued Friday.

Investigators have pointed to hackers linked to North Korea as responsible for the attack. Binance suspended user withdrawals following the incident, but said in its latest update that they were on track to resume Sunday. Prices for most major cryptocurrencies fell after news of the cyberattack.

The breach comes amid wider concerns expressed by observers that increasingly capable artificial intelligence models could put cryptography and information technology security protocols at risk. President Donald Trump said Friday that the United States was looking into the hack. The FBI said it was working with relevant public and private partners to understand the scope of the activity and was prepared to investigate.
