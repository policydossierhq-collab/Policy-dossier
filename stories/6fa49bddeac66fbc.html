Heat wave: Anambra first lady urges residents to take precautions

2026-10-25

Anambra First Lady Nonye Soludo has urged residents to take precautions as the heat wave intensifies. She cited staying hydrated as one way to help prevent heat-related illnesses.

The appeal highlights the risk of illness during the heat wave and encourages residents to take steps to reduce that risk. The supplied report does not describe specific illnesses or their symptoms.

Hydration is the only precaution named in the report. It does not provide further recommendations or details about the heat wave.
