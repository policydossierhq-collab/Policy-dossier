Nasarawa Assembly decries resurgence of kidnappings

2026-10-27

The Nasarawa State House of Assembly has urged stronger security measures following reports of renewed kidnappings in Lafia and other parts of the state. Speaker Danladi Jatau raised the concern during Tuesday’s plenary in Lafia, responding to a matter presented by Lafia Central representative Solomon Yakubu Akwashiki.

Akwashiki told lawmakers that two people had been abducted in Agyaragu Lafia the previous day, while people were also kidnapped in Agunji Lafia a few days earlier. He said the two people taken in the more recent incident remained with their abductors, who were demanding ransom. He called on the state government and security agencies to act to protect residents and property.

Jatau said the House would treat the matter seriously, citing fear among residents and disruption to economic activity. The Assembly urged Governor Abdullahi Sule to direct security agencies, including the police, the Department of State Services and the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps, to strengthen efforts across the state. It also called for traditional leaders and vigilantes to work together to identify unfamiliar people in their communities.

The Speaker directed the Clerk of the House to send the resolution to the governor for action. Lawmakers who supported the motion said kidnappings were affecting their constituents and called for more decisive measures by state and federal authorities. They also urged both levels of government to procure more sophisticated weapons and deploy technology to address insecurity.
