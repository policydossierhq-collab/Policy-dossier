Tinubu, Atiku, Obi Will Collapse Under Their Own Weight — SDP’s Adewole

2026-11-09

Social Democratic Party presidential candidate Adewole Adebayo said he was confident of winning a free, fair and credible election in 2027. He argued that President Bola Tinubu, Atiku Abubakar and Peter Obi would fall under their own weight, though he said his focus was defeating poverty and insecurity rather than simply defeating political opponents.

In a television interview on Monday, Adebayo presented his manifesto, titled “Farewell to Poverty and Insecurity,” as a plan for progress and good governance that he said could improve Nigerians’ lives. He described electoral victory as persuading a majority of citizens to support the plan, rather than a contest in which opponents are defeated physically.

Adebayo said that overcoming poverty and insecurity would also require addressing what he characterised as voter ignorance, fatigue and apathy. He urged citizens not to vote based only on sentiment and challenged the belief that individual votes do not count. He said greater public engagement would help voters assess and support his proposed approach.

On security, Adebayo argued that fixing government would help resolve the country’s security challenges. He said Nigerians should no longer see terrorists within six months, citing the capacity of the Nigerian Armed Forces, and pledged to address banditry. He also promised that citizens would receive security as a “Christmas present” in 2027. The presidential election is scheduled for January 16, 2027.
