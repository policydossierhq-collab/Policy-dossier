You Cannot Negotiate For TUC, Osifo Replies Ajearo As N70,000 Controversy Drags On

2026-11-02

Trade Union of Nigeria (TUC) President Festus Osifo has challenged Nigerian Labour Congress (NLC) President Joe Ajaero over who can negotiate workers’ pay and allowances with the Federal Government. The dispute follows an agreement on a N70,000 transportation allowance approved by President Bola Tinubu. Osifo said the TUC was not included in the negotiations and called the agreement an aberration.

Speaking on Monday, Osifo argued that neither union should negotiate on behalf of the other without its participation or consultation. He said the TUC and NLC must come together for negotiations, while acknowledging that the NLC has more affiliate unions. Osifo challenged Ajaero to produce an International Labour Organization regulation authorising one body to negotiate without the other.

Osifo also disputed Ajaero’s reliance on the Joint Public Sector Negotiating Council (JNC), saying the meeting that produced the allowance agreement was not a JNC meeting. He said the council has membership and quorum requirements, and that its members were not present. Osifo added that the labour minister and Ajaero were not council members.

He said the JNC should consider consequential adjustments after changes to the minimum wage. Osifo criticised the use of a questionnaire asking the council to apply N40,000 across the board—the difference between the previous N30,000 minimum wage and the new N70,000 figure. He said he had raised his objection directly with Ajaero, arguing that the approach had left federal workers earning very low salaries.
