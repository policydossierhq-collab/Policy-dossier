Katsina APC declares Funtua campaign peaceful amid reports of attacks in Malumfashi

2026-10-29

The All Progressives Congress in Katsina State described Wednesday’s Tinubu–Dikko 2027 campaign launch in Funtua as peaceful, saying no clashes, injuries or property losses were recorded at the Funtua Township Stadium. The party’s state chairman, Bishir Saulawa, thanked Governor Dikko Radda, security agencies, party members and other stakeholders for their conduct at the event.

Separate reports described attacks on supporters travelling to Funtua through Malumfashi Local Government Area. Some supporters alleged that groups of youths threw stones and bottles and used other weapons. Those affected reported injuries and lost belongings, while some vehicles had damaged windscreens. Stones were also reported around the Malumfashi town roundabout, near the road linking Bakori Local Government Area.

The reported incidents were along the route to the campaign, not necessarily at the stadium. The APC chairman’s statement did not address the allegations or clarify whether his account covered supporters travelling to the event. The number of people injured, the extent of the damage and who carried out the attacks have not been established.

At the launch, Radda urged residents to support President Bola Tinubu and APC candidates in the 2027 general elections. He identified security, infrastructure, education, healthcare and economic opportunities as priorities, and called on candidates and supporters to campaign peacefully and respect voters’ choices. Deputy Governor Faruk Jobe, former governors Aminu Masari and Ibrahim Shema, lawmakers and other party stakeholders attended. Saulawa presented party flags and banners to candidates for three Senate seats, 15 House of Representatives seats and 34 State Assembly seats.
