CAF picks Nigeria’s Samson Adamu as new General Secretary

2026-11-01

The Confederation of African Football has appointed Nigerian Samson Adamu as its General Secretary following a recruitment process that included advertising the role and interviewing several candidates. CAF announced the appointment on Sunday.

Adamu had been serving as acting General Secretary since March, after Veron Mosengo-Omba resigned. Mosengo-Omba later became president of the football federation in the Democratic Republic of Congo. CAF President Patrice Motsepe said the appointment followed a competitive selection process.

Adamu has worked at CAF for eight years. He joined in 2018 as Director of Competitions and later became Director of Tournaments and Events. In those roles, he contributed to the delivery of major continental competitions, including the Africa Cup of Nations. He is also a graduate of the FIFA Master programme in International Management, Law and Humanities of Sport.

In his new position, Adamu is expected to oversee CAF’s day-to-day administration and support the organisation’s strategic priorities. He is the son of Amos Adamu, a former Director of Sports in Nigeria’s then Ministry of Sports and a former FIFA Executive Committee member.

Adamu joins a succession of CAF General Secretaries that has included three members of Egypt’s Fahmy family. Mourad Fahmy held the post from 1961 to 1982, followed by his son Mustapha Fahmy from 1982 to 2010. Amr Fahmy, Mourad’s grandson, served from 2017 to 2019.
