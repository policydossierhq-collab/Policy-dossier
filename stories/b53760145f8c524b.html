Sultan Of Sokoto To Turban Peter Igho As Kakakin Daular Usmaniyya

2026-10-27

The Sultan of Sokoto, Alhaji Muhammad Sa’ad Abubakar III, is scheduled to formally turban veteran broadcaster and television producer Chief Peter Igho as Kakakin Daular Usmaniyya on Sunday, November 1, 2026. The ceremony will take place at the Sultan’s Palace in Sokoto, according to a statement from the palace.

Igho was conferred with the traditional title on September 10, 2025. The honour recognises his contributions to the development of the Sultanate Council, Sokoto State and Nigeria. The ceremony is expected to draw prominent Nigerians from across the country and from different walks of life, with the stated aim of strengthening cordial relations and promoting unity among the country’s diverse communities.

Igho is a former Executive Director at the Nigerian Television Authority and is known for producing and directing *Cock Crow at Dawn*, described as one of Nigeria’s pioneering television dramas. His work on the programme and other productions helped shape the country’s broadcasting industry. He has received national and international awards for his contributions to broadcasting and mentored generations of media professionals. A native of the Niger Delta, he is also recognised for cultural advocacy, community leadership and philanthropy.

Sultan Abubakar, who will preside over the ceremony, is the 20th occupant of the Sokoto throne and President-General of the Nigerian Supreme Council for Islamic Affairs. A retired Brigadier-General of the Nigerian Army, he ascended the throne on November 2, 2006, and is known for advocating peace, interfaith dialogue, education and national unity.

The Sultanate’s heritage is linked to the Sokoto Caliphate, also known as Daular Usmaniyya, founded in 1804 by Islamic scholar and reformer Shehu Usman Dan Fodio. The Sultanate Council remains a custodian of that heritage and an institution associated with Islamic leadership, peacebuilding and national cohesion in Nigeria.
