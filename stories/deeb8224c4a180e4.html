Tinubu expands 2027 campaign itinerary to 19 states

2026-11-19

President Bola Tinubu’s planned itinerary for the 2027 presidential campaign has expanded from 12 states to 19, with the Federal Capital Territory also included. The revised All Progressives Congress calendar lists 20 rallies between October 24, 2026, and January 13, 2027. Tinubu previously said he would not be able to attend every campaign rally.

The itinerary adds Katsina, Taraba, Zamfara, Imo and Kwara, and includes Akwa Ibom, Jigawa and Kebbi, where Tinubu has already campaigned. He attended rallies in Uyo on November 6, Port Harcourt on November 7, Dutse on November 10 and Birnin Kebbi on November 17.

Upcoming stops include Katsina on December 2, Kano on December 17, Jalingo in Taraba State on December 19 and Abuja on December 21. The schedule then lists Gusau in Zamfara State on December 28 and Owerri in Imo State on December 30. Campaigning is set to resume in the new year in Yola on January 4, Sokoto on January 5 and Ilorin on January 8, followed by Asaba on January 11. Lagos is scheduled to host the final rally on January 13.

The revised calendar moves the Sokoto event from December 5 to January 5 and shifts the Lagos finale from January 9 to January 13. By region, the North-West has the most scheduled state visits, with seven. The North-East and South-South have three each, while the North-Central, South-West and South-East have two apiece.
