Breaking: Harry Kane crowned 2026 Ballon d’Or winner

2026-10-26

England captain Harry Kane has won the 2026 Ballon d’Or, football’s leading individual award. The Bayern Munich striker received the honour at a ceremony on Monday, October 26, following a strong year for club and country.

Kane’s award recognises his performances for Bayern and the England national team. He has combined goalscoring with creating chances for teammates, establishing himself as one of the world’s leading forwards.

He prevailed over a field that included Paris Saint-Germain’s Ousmane Dembélé, Real Madrid’s Kylian Mbappé and Barcelona’s Lamine Yamal. The Ballon d’Or, organised by France Football, recognises the best footballer over the relevant assessment period. Kane’s win adds to a career in which he has become England’s all-time leading goalscorer and one of Europe’s most prolific strikers.
