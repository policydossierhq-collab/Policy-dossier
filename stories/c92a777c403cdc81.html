Nigerian stocks gain N307 billion as Transcorp Power, banks drive market rally

2026-11-04

Nigerian stocks gained approximately N307.4 billion on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, as buying in Transcorp Power and major banking shares lifted the broader market. The NGX All-Share Index advanced 0.19% to 254,762.81 points, from 254,289.35 in the previous session, while market capitalisation rose to N165.42 trillion.

Transcorp Power led the session’s gainers, rising 9.97%. GTCO added 5.1%, UBA gained 2.68% and Zenith Bank rose 2.65%. Honeywell Flour increased 4.04% to N24.45, while Transcorp edged up 0.17% to N30.05. The Banking Index climbed 0.72% to 2,403.98 points, making banking the strongest-performing sector reported.

The advance came despite losses in several shares. Eterna fell 9.94% to N38.50, the session’s steepest decline, while Sovereign Trust Insurance and Learn Africa each lost nearly 10%. Overall market breadth was positive: 27 stocks advanced, compared with 23 that declined.

Investors traded 552.31 million shares worth N37.46 billion in 32,874 transactions. Share volume and transaction value increased, but the number of deals fell 15.88%. The session extended Tuesday’s N28.65 billion gain, which had recovered most of Monday’s losses. The market’s year-to-date return rose to 63.72%, and its month-to-date return reached 0.2%. Gains in major stocks supported the rally, while declines in other counters left the overall performance mixed.
