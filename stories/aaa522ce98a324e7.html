Minister demands results from colleges of education under dual mandate

2026-10-29

Education Minister Tunji Alausa has urged provosts of colleges of education to demonstrate results under the dual-mandate policy, with stronger accountability and improved teacher preparation. He said the reform could strengthen colleges and widen opportunities for academic and professional advancement, but would require strategic leadership and cooperation among stakeholders.

Alausa’s remarks were delivered by Dr Cecilia Uba, the Federal Ministry of Education’s Director of Colleges of Education, at the National Commission for Colleges of Education Teacher Education Leadership and Impact Award ceremony in Abuja on Thursday. The event, held in partnership with Gosima Group, was part of the commission’s retreat and workshop on implementing the dual mandate and its strategic plan. The policy allows eligible colleges to offer both the Nigeria Certificate in Education and approved bachelor’s degree programmes in education.

The minister reaffirmed the ministry’s commitment to working with the NCCE, provosts and other stakeholders to improve the quality and relevance of teacher education. He also said award recipients should treat the recognition as a call to greater responsibility, innovation and service. NCCE Executive Secretary Dr Angela Ajala urged colleges to improve performance, strengthen teacher preparation, support research and adopt digital innovation, while pursuing academic excellence and financial sustainability.

Former Senate President Anyim Pius Anyim said teacher education was important to national development and called for modern facilities, sustainable funding and stronger professional development. Gosima Group President and Group Managing Director Dr Gideon Chidiebere Osi said the company planned to expand scholarships for deserving students through its charitable foundation. He said its partnership with the NCCE would support educational leadership and teacher education through scholarships, mentorship and youth leadership development.
