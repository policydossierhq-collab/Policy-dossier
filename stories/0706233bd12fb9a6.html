Chelsea’s Joao Pedro ruled out with thigh injury

2026-10-27

Chelsea forward Joao Pedro is expected to be out for “a few weeks” with a thigh injury, head coach Xabi Alonso said ahead of Wednesday’s Carabao Cup match against Liverpool. The Brazilian missed Chelsea’s defeat to Tottenham because of the injury and is not ready to play in the team’s next matches. Alonso said he could not give a more precise estimate for his return.

Alonso called Joao Pedro an important player and said Chelsea would feel his absence during a demanding run of fixtures. He expressed confidence, however, that the squad had enough options to compete, with the team focused on preparing for Liverpool and finding solutions among the players available.

Chelsea will also be without Cole Palmer for the Liverpool trip. The England international was sent off against Tottenham and is unavailable through suspension. Alonso said he had discussed the incident with Palmer, who understood that the mistake could have been avoided. He said he hoped it would not happen again.

Despite the suspension, Alonso said Palmer was in a good place with his teammates and that the club wanted him to enjoy his football. The coach’s comments came as Chelsea prepared to face Liverpool without two players who will be unavailable for the cup tie.
