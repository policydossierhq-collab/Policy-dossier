NDLEA arrests South African woman with 36.65kg of cocaine at Lagos airport

2026-10-25

The National Drug Law Enforcement Agency says it arrested a 34-year-old South African woman at Murtala Muhammed International Airport in Lagos with 36.65kg of cocaine. Qhina Beauty was detained on October 7 as she prepared to board a Qatar Airways flight to South Africa via Doha. Officers found 30 blocks of cocaine in her two travel bags, concealed in seven parcels with dried pepper. The agency said she told investigators she had arrived in Nigeria on September 30 to collect the drugs.

Further inquiries led to the arrest of car dealer Uchenna Victor Umunnakwe, 46, on October 14 in Lagos. The agency said he admitted receiving Beauty at the airport, arranging her accommodation at two hotels and moving her to a hotel near the airport before her departure.

In a separate airport operation, officers arrested Anetor Gloria Ubong, a 62-year-old realtor based in Texas, after she arrived from Houston on October 21. A search of her checked luggage uncovered 18 parcels of cannabis known as loud, weighing 9.45kg and wrapped in gift paper. The agency said Ubong, who has Nigerian and US passports, told investigators the box had been given to her in the United States for delivery to her daughter’s fiancé in Nigeria.

The agency also reported seizures and arrests elsewhere. In Lagos, officers found diazepam and tramadol tablets, 3.7kg of skunk and 230.25 litres of a locally produced alcoholic-drug mixture called skuchies at the residence of 41-year-old Eletu Rasheedat. In Warri, Delta State, they arrested Peter Ugochukwu, 51, and seized thousands of tramadol capsules and tablets, codeine syrup, Rohypnol and other substances, including injectable drugs. A separate arrest in Warri led to the seizure of 6kg of skunk from Helen Bakporo Samson, 46.

In Ibadan, Oyo State, NDLEA operatives arrested Fajana Dada Oluwaseyi, 41. The agency said officers had previously seized 197.8kg of cannabis from his residence on September 16, after which he escaped arrest. The latest operations are part of the agency’s stated effort to disrupt drug trafficking and seize illicit substances across Nigeria.
