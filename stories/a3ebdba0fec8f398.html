Opebi-Mende-Ojota Link Bridge To Be Opened Before Christmas, Says Sanwo-Olu

2026-10-25

Lagos State Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu has confirmed that the construction of the Opebi-Mende Link Bridge is approximately 80% complete. He expressed confidence that the bridge will be opened to the public before Christmas, despite the project exceeding its original September completion timeline.

During a recent site inspection, Governor Sanwo-Olu acknowledged the reasons for the delay while emphasizing the importance of maintaining the project's unique architectural and engineering standards. He highlighted the bridge's steel framework, which is designed to evoke the region's historical connection to cane weaving, thus ensuring cultural preservation in its design.

The construction involves the installation of over 300 tonnes of steel at heights reaching up to 25 meters. Governor Sanwo-Olu reported that three out of four major steel components have been completed, while work continues on the final component. Additionally, the bridge will feature light-emitting diode installations in the colours of Lagos State, enhancing its visual appeal.

Despite the setbacks, the governor reassured that the quality of engineering and public safety would remain a priority over speed. Once completed, the bridge will undergo site cleaning, kerb repainting, restoration of affected areas, and landscaping before it opens.

This infrastructure project, commissioned in 2022, aims to alleviate traffic congestion in Ikeja and surrounding areas. Governor Sanwo-Olu anticipates that the bridge will not only ease transportation but will also become a hub for recreation, culture, and tourism, enriching community identity and history. He views it as a significant addition to Lagos' infrastructure landscape, merging innovation with cultural significance.
