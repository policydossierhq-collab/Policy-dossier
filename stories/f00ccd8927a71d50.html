Ballon d’Or: Six journalists rank Messi first

2026-10-31

Lionel Messi placed fifth in the 2026 men’s Ballon d’Or standings, but journalists from six countries ranked him first. The voting breakdown showed that representatives from Argentina, Brazil, Belarus, China, Panama and the United Arab Emirates gave the Inter Miami forward their top selection. Each first-place vote was worth 15 points, contributing to Messi’s total of 612.

Brazil’s first-place vote was a notable choice given the country’s longstanding football rivalry with Argentina. Messi, aged 39, finished behind winner Harry Kane, Kylian Mbappé, Lamine Yamal and Rodri. Kane secured the award with 1,055 points, while Mbappé took second with 1,027. Yamal earned 881 points and Rodri received 726.

The 28-point gap between Kane and Mbappé made the result the fourth-closest men’s Ballon d’Or vote in the competition’s history. Messi’s six top rankings exceeded the number received by Ousmane Dembélé, the 2025 winner, who did not finish first on any of the 100 ballots. Dembélé placed seventh overall with 418 points.

Journalists from Burkina Faso, China, France, Georgia, Haiti, North Macedonia and Nigeria ranked Dembélé second. The voting figures show that while Messi did not contend for the top position in the final standings, he still received the highest ranking from voters in six different countries.
