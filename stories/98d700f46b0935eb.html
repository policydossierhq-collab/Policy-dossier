Atiku Will Be On Ballot In 2027 And Will Beat Tinubu Silly — ADC

2026-11-18

The African Democratic Congress (ADC) says Atiku Abubakar will remain its presidential candidate for the 2027 general election and defeat President Bola Tinubu. The claim was made by Phrank Shaibu, the party’s director of strategic communication, as the ADC contests a court judgment that it says has put its leadership and election preparations in legal uncertainty.

The dispute follows a November 5, 2026 judgment by Justice Peter Lifu of the Federal High Court in Abuja in a case brought by Bala Gombe. The ADC has filed a 17-ground appeal, arguing that the trial court lacked jurisdiction, that Gombe did not have standing to bring the case, and that the court mishandled disputed evidence and issued orders that should not stand.

The party has asked the Court of Appeal to prevent the Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) from changing its leadership records, Atiku’s nomination particulars or the records of its governorship candidates while the appeal is pending. The ADC argues that implementing the judgment before the appeal is decided could create uncertainty for its candidates and undermine the practical effect of a later ruling in its favour.

The party is also challenging a July 24, 2026 ruling that allowed Gombe to amend his originating summons while its objection to the trial court’s jurisdiction remained unresolved. The ADC says the amendment changed the description of parties and the capacity in which a defendant was sued without prior leave. It is asking the appellate court to set aside that order and determine the jurisdictional objection. The party says its appeals are intended to preserve its existing leadership and candidate records until the courts resolve the dispute.
