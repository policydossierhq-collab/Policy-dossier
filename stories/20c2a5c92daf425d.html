NGX rallies as equity market cap reaches record N163.1 trillion

2026-10-26

The Nigerian Exchange’s equity market capitalisation rose by N2.64 trillion on Monday, October 26, to a record N163.16 trillion. It had closed at N160.52 trillion on Friday, October 23. Monday’s total also surpassed the previous record of N163.06 trillion, set on September 23.

The NGX All-Share Index gained 4,068.27 points, or 1.65%, to close at a record 251,290.54 points, up from 247,222.27 in the previous session. The market’s advance came as gains in industrial and banking shares outweighed losses in some individual stocks.

The strongest sector move came from the NGX Industrial Index, which climbed 5.37% to 11,218.05 points from 10,646.68. Dangote Cement was a key driver, rising by the maximum permitted 10% for the session. Its share price added N111.10 to close at N1,222.10, lifting the company’s market capitalisation to N20.62 trillion.

Other sectors also advanced. The NGX Oil/Gas Index rose 0.88% to 6,089.08 points, supported by a 2.48% gain in Aradel Holdings, which closed at N1,439.90. The Insurance Index added 0.67% to reach 1,056.47 points, with AIICO Insurance up 4.18% and Coronation Insurance gaining 2.88%. Profit-taking weighed on some stocks, including ABC Transport, down 9.59%, and Caverton Offshore Support Group, down 9.38%. Select consumer-facing and specialised segments also recorded mild pullbacks.
