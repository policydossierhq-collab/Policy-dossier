2027: Abia CP warns politicians against violence, intimidation

2026-10-29

Abia State Commissioner of Police Wilfred Afolabi has urged political parties and their supporters to avoid violence, intimidation, inflammatory statements and property damage ahead of the 2027 general elections. He also asked parties to notify police in writing before holding rallies, processions and other political activities so security arrangements can be made.

Afolabi spoke at a peace accord signing in Umuahia attended by the Independent National Electoral Commission’s Resident Electoral Commissioner, Prof. Sylvia Agu, representatives of security agencies, registered political parties and campaign teams. The accord was read to participants after the police command’s legal officer reviewed relevant electoral laws. Party representatives then signed, pledging to pursue their political ambitions peacefully.

The police commissioner said the command was committed to providing professional and impartial security for all candidates, parties and supporters. He said police had strengthened security through intelligence gathering and analysis, crime mapping, a revamped Cybercrime Unit and improved operational response. Afolabi also acknowledged Governor Alex Otti’s directive on security for campaigns and related activities, and pledged continued cooperation with INEC, security agencies and political stakeholders to protect lives and property.

Agu said INEC would serve all candidates equally and provide a level playing field. She urged candidates to maintain peace before, during and after the election, and to accept the outcome of a free, fair and credible poll. Those dissatisfied with the results, she said, should seek redress through the courts rather than resort to violence or undermine the electoral process. Agu said the accord was intended to prevent conflict by committing signatories to peaceful conduct throughout the election period.
