Elon Musk loses $53.9 billion in one week as net worth drops to $849.7 billion

2026-11-08

Elon Musk’s estimated net worth fell by $53.9 billion over seven days to $849.7 billion, according to billionaire rankings updated on Sunday, November 8, 2026. The drop was a 5.97% reduction from the approximately $903.6 billion estimate recorded a week earlier. Musk remained the world’s richest person despite the decline.

His fortune was still $471.6 billion greater than that of Amazon founder Jeff Bezos, ranked second with an estimated $378.1 billion. Google co-founder Larry Page was third at $236.9 billion, followed by Sergey Brin at $218.1 billion and Meta chief executive Mark Zuckerberg at $209.7 billion.

Musk’s wealth is largely tied to his stakes in Tesla, SpaceX and other businesses, making its estimated value sensitive to changes in company valuations and market conditions. The latest decline came during a turbulent period for technology stocks, including companies associated with artificial intelligence, electric vehicles and space exploration.

The weekly loss follows sharp movements in the opposite direction. On October 21, Musk’s estimated fortune rose by $88 billion in a single day, reaching $1.013 trillion amid renewed investor optimism about SpaceX’s financial performance and expectations ahead of Tesla’s quarterly earnings announcement. His wealth also increased by $30.7 billion in 24 hours in August, reaching $894.9 billion as valuations across major holdings improved. The swings illustrate how quickly billionaire wealth estimates can change as the value of their business interests shifts.
