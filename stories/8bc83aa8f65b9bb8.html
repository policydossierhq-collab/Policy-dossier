Tinubu urges Igboho to work with security agencies to protect South-West

2026-11-23

President Bola Tinubu has urged Yoruba activist Sunday Adeyemo, known as Sunday Igboho, to work with government security agencies to improve safety across the South-West, according to a statement from Igboho’s media office. Igboho is the founder of the Iru Ekun Security Network, a home-grown security initiative.

The statement said Tinubu spoke with Igboho inside his official car after an All Progressives Congress presidential campaign rally at Mapo Hall in Ibadan on Saturday. The meeting focused on Iru Ekun’s activities and the need for its operatives to coordinate with the police, Nigerian Army, Department of State Services and other relevant bodies. Igboho’s media office issued the account on Monday in Ibadan, Oyo State.

According to the statement, Tinubu called for Iru Ekun to continue supporting security in rural communities, urban centres and forests across Yorubaland. It said the president highlighted the importance of cooperation in addressing criminality in farming settlements and forest reserves, and of monitoring areas to guard against what the statement described as possible infiltration by terrorists from Lake Chad, Mali and other African countries.

The account also said Iru Ekun was asked to provide security to Nigerians within its areas of operation regardless of ethnicity, religion or political affiliation. It added that the network should inform security agencies before carrying out its security duties, with the aim of improving coordination and public confidence in the government’s responsibility to protect residents. The appeal for cooperation comes ahead of the January 2027 general elections, which the statement cited as a period when efforts to address insecurity in Yoruba states would be important.
