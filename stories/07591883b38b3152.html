Police arrest fake lawyer after 11 years of practice in Ebonyi

2026-10-28

Police in Ebonyi State have arrested Ugwumba Enyinnaya, who they allege posed as a lawyer and practised for more than 11 years. The arrest followed an investigation into a petition alleging that he obtained money by false pretence, police spokesperson SP Joshua Ukandu said in a statement on Tuesday.

Police said the petitioner, Chukwu David, alleged that Enyinnaya took ₦650,000 from him in March 2026 to help process and secure approval for street naming in Ohaozara Local Government Area. David also alleged that Enyinnaya presented himself as a lawyer with years of professional experience.

According to the police, Enyinnaya admitted during questioning that he was not a lawyer. A search of his apartment led to the recovery of items police described as exhibits, including a Nigerian Bar Association seal, barrister’s robes and wig, and a portrait of the suspect wearing legal attire. Officers also reportedly found purported LL.B. and LL.M. certificates bearing Enyinnaya’s name and that of Enugu State University of Science and Technology, as well as court judgments and legal documents, including powers of attorney and deeds of conveyance.

Police said Enyinnaya had been charged to court. Ebonyi Commissioner of Police Hope Okafor praised the officers involved and urged residents to check the credentials of people offering legal services and report suspicious activity to police.
