Gov Lawal pledges more projects as APC campaign storms Shinkafi LGA

2026-11-28

Zamfara State Governor Dauda Lawal pledged to deliver more projects in Shinkafi Local Government Area while speaking at the APC Zamfara North campaign flag-off rally on Friday. The event was held at the Eid ground in Shinkafi, where the governor also asked residents to support the party in the 2027 general election.

The rally drew senior party figures, including former governor Abdul’aziz Yari, who is director general of the Tinubu/Shettima Presidential Campaign Council; former governor Mahmuda Aliyu Shinkafi; and Zamfara APC Chairman Kabiru Moyi. Tens of thousands of supporters welcomed the entourage at the city gate, chanting slogans in support of the party.

Lawal thanked Shinkafi residents for their solidarity and reception. He said his administration had made progress in education, healthcare, infrastructure and the economy, and pointed to the referral hospital under construction in Shinkafi as a major project. He described it as a legacy project and said it would mark a turning point for healthcare in Zamfara and the North West.

The governor also said his administration had rehabilitated six primary healthcare centres, including facilities in Badarawa, Birnin Yero, Galadi, Jangeru and Kurya. He reported that 22 of 31 projects had been completed over the previous three and a half years, with the remaining nine ongoing and more than 80 per cent complete. Lawal urged voters to support President Bola Ahmed Tinubu, himself and the APC in the upcoming election.
