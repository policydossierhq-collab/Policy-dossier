Watford sack Dionisi after just 18 games

2026-11-22

Watford have dismissed head coach Alessio Dionisi after 18 games in charge, a day after the Championship side lost 4-0 to Sheffield United. The club confirmed the Italian’s departure on Sunday and said it had started the search for a replacement. Watford are 21st in the table, with four league victories under Dionisi.

Sporting director Luke Dowling thanked Dionisi and his staff for their professionalism and efforts, and wished them well. He said the club hoped to be able to announce a successor shortly. Dionisi, 46, was appointed in June after Ed Still left following three months at Vicarage Road.

Dionisi’s tenure opened with a win over Southampton, but Watford then managed only two victories in their next 12 matches in all competitions. A win against Charlton on October 17 was followed by defeats to Wolves and Norwich, before the heavy loss at Sheffield United.

Before joining Watford, Dionisi managed Italian Serie B club Empoli. He was dismissed there in March after five months, during which the team recorded five wins in 22 matches. His Watford appointment was his first managerial role outside Italy.

His departure adds to a period of frequent coaching changes at the club. Since Tom Cleverley left at the end of the 2024-25 season, Watford have appointed Ed Still, Javi Gracia, Paulo Pezzolano and Dionisi as permanent head coaches; Charlie Daniels also took charge for two matches as interim boss. Dionisi was the club’s 12th head coach, excluding interim appointments, since the end of the 2020-21 season. Watford are now seeking their 25th manager since the Pozzo family took over in 2012.
