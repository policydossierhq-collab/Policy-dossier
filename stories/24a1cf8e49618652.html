2027: Lagos voters laud ‘smooth’ INEC mock exercise

2026-11-28

Lagos voters praised the Independent National Electoral Commission’s (INEC) mock accreditation and result-upload exercise, reporting prompt arrivals by officials and smooth operation of the Bimodal Voter Accreditation System (BVAS). They urged the commission to deliver the same experience during the 2027 general elections.

At Polling Unit 009 in Yaba, voter Bidemi Adeboye said he arrived at 8 a.m. to find officials already present and completed accreditation and voting in about two minutes. He said the BVAS verified his details after he presented his Permanent Voter Card. The mock ballot papers used at the unit showed coloured options rather than political party names or logos.

Alhaja Mojisola Adewale, a voter in her seventies, also described the process as orderly and said accreditation and voting were quick. At another Yaba polling unit, Ademuyiwa Adenrele, the All Progressives Congress local government youth leader, said officials arrived on time and voters were being processed in under a minute. An INEC official at Polling Unit 009 said the exercise had been smooth since the unit opened at 8:30 a.m. The official reported that 10 of 1,632 registered voters had taken part by 10:40 a.m.

INEC is conducting the exercise across the country to test equipment and result systems under live conditions ahead of the 2027 elections. In Lagos, it is taking place at 490 polling units in 245 registration areas. The commission’s chairman, Joash Amupitan, said the exercise would test upgraded BVAS devices and safeguards on the INEC Result Viewing Portal, including checks intended to prevent arithmetic inconsistencies and over-voting. He also described it as a rehearsal for security and operational arrangements.

The presidential and National Assembly elections are scheduled for Jan. 16, 2027, with governorship and State Houses of Assembly elections set for Feb. 6. Voters said INEC’s performance in the mock exercise should be sustained and replicated across polling units on election days.
