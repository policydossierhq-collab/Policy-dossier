Süper Lig: Osimhen scores again as Galatasaray thrash Konyaspor

2026-10-31

Victor Osimhen scored again as Galatasaray defeated Konyaspor 4-0 on Friday to strengthen their hold on top spot in the Turkish Süper Lig. The win took Okan Buruk’s side to 22 points after 10 matches, with Osimhen’s goal his ninth in eight league appearances this season.

The Nigerian striker opened the scoring in the 12th minute, heading in Gabriel Sara’s free kick. He nearly added another in first-half stoppage time, but his attempt from the left side of the penalty area was tipped over the crossbar. Yunus Akgün made it 2-0 in the 28th minute, finishing from outside the box after a corner worked by Sara and Batrakov.

Galatasaray extended their lead soon after the break when Aleksey Batrakov collected Sara’s pass inside the area and scored in the 49th minute, his first league goal for the club. Six minutes later, VAR overturned referee Cihan Aydın’s decision to award a corner following an incident involving Rafael Leão. Leão converted the resulting penalty to complete the scoring and register his first goal for Galatasaray.

Osimhen was substituted in the 61st minute after recording five shots, including three on target. The result was significant given Galatasaray had lost four of their previous six visits to Konyaspor, including a 2-0 defeat on their last trip. Konyaspor defender Chidozie Awaziem played the full match. He made two interceptions, three clearances and three recoveries, won three of four ground duels and completed 97 per cent of his passes.

Galatasaray have now won eight of their 10 league matches and are due to host VfB Stuttgart in the UEFA Champions League on Tuesday, 3 November.
