Lagos govt on alert, says no Ebola case in state

2026-10-25

The Lagos State Government says there are no confirmed Ebola cases in the state and is strengthening its preparedness after an imported case of Ebola Bundibugyo virus was confirmed in Abuja. Commissioner for Health Prof. Akin Abayomi urged residents to remain calm, vigilant and alert to official information.

The state Ministry of Health is intensifying disease surveillance, risk communication and community engagement through its Directorate of Epidemiology, Biosecurity and Global Health. It has also assessed preparedness at Murtala Muhammed International Airport with airport management, Port Health Services and other aviation and public health stakeholders. The work focused on passenger screening, information sharing, infection prevention and control, and emergency response coordination.

Ebola can spread through direct contact with the blood or other body fluids of an infected person who has symptoms, contaminated materials, or the bodies of people who have died from the disease. Contact with infected animals can also transmit the virus. Symptoms may appear between two and 21 days after exposure and can include fever, weakness, headache, muscle pain, sore throat, vomiting, diarrhoea and abdominal pain. Unexplained bleeding or bruising may occur in some cases.

Abayomi advised people who become unwell, especially after possible exposure or travel from an area with active transmission, to seek prompt medical assessment. Residents with fever should take a malaria rapid diagnostic test before using antimalarial medicine; if the test is negative, a health professional should assess other possible causes. He also advised regular handwashing, avoiding contact with sick people’s body fluids and potentially contaminated materials, and avoiding sick or dead wild animals.

Travellers arriving in Nigeria were urged to cooperate with Port Health Services, provide accurate travel and health information, and follow public health guidance. Anyone who suspects exposure or develops symptoms during travel or after arrival should contact public health personnel for advice before visiting a healthcare facility. Suspected cases or exposure concerns in Lagos can be reported by calling 112 or 767, or the state health ministry’s Directorate of Epidemiology, Biosecurity and Global Health on 08023169485. The government says it will continue monitoring developments and provide verified updates.
