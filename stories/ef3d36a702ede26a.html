Ebola: NCDC expands health declaration requirement for international travellers

2026-10-25

Nigeria’s National Centre for Disease Control (NCDC) is expanding the health declaration requirement for international travellers in connection with Ebola, according to the report. The agency has sent advisories to the Federal Airports Authority of Nigeria (FAAN) and the Nigerian Civil Aviation Authority (NCAA).

Further instructions are expected to guide how the requirement will be implemented in collaboration with airlines. The available information does not specify which travellers or routes are covered, when the expanded requirement will take effect, or what information passengers will be asked to provide.
