Mutfwang Vows To Track Down Gero Killers, Urges Communities To Remain Vigilant

2026-10-27

Plateau State Governor Caleb Mutfwang has vowed to pursue those responsible for a deadly attack in Gero, in Jos South Local Government Area, and urged residents to share timely information with security agencies. Speaking on Tuesday at the swearing-in of Special Advisers at the New Government House in Jos, he described the attack, which occurred on Monday night, as disturbing, particularly because it was close to Jos metropolis. He said investigators had recovered evidence and were following leads.

Mutfwang urged communities across the state to remain vigilant, saying security forces relied on residents’ information to respond to threats. He also said the alleged leader of an attack on Kombun District in Mangu Local Government Area had been arrested, and that his administration would continue efforts to prosecute suspects linked to violence.

The Plateau State Police Command confirmed six deaths in Gero, revising an earlier figure of seven reported by community representatives. Police spokesperson SP Alfred Alabo said unidentified armed men opened fire in the community in the early hours of Tuesday. Mobile police and tactical officers, working with military personnel and Operation Rainbow, dislodged the attackers, who fled into nearby hillside forest. The victims’ bodies were taken to a morgue and an injured person was transported to hospital.

Alabo said joint security forces had launched search and clearance operations, which remained ongoing, and recovered motorcycles and other items allegedly abandoned by the attackers. Commissioner of Police Ayodeji Faniyan visited the community and met local leaders and residents. He called for stronger cooperation on intelligence gathering and urged people to report suspicious activity, while community representatives appealed for measures to prevent further violence and a sustained security presence.
