Osimhen welcomes second child with partner Stefanie

2026-10-30

Nigeria and Galatasaray striker Victor Osimhen and his partner, Stefanie Kim Ladewig, have welcomed their second child, a daughter named Liora Audrey. The Turkish club announced the birth and congratulated the couple, wishing the newborn a long, healthy and happy life.

The announcement came about two weeks after Ladewig, a model, escaped injury in a three-vehicle crash in Istanbul while eight months pregnant. The accident occurred on 13 October in the Mithatpasa neighbourhood of Kemerburgaz, in the city’s Eyupsultan district.

Reports said Ladewig was driving an SUV when it collided with a light commercial vehicle and then veered into another SUV travelling in the opposite direction. The vehicles were damaged, but no injuries were reported. Ladewig was taken to a private hospital in the Maslak area for precautionary checks because of her pregnancy and was later discharged.

Osimhen, 27, is a member of Nigeria’s Super Eagles and plays for Galatasaray in the Turkish Süper Lig. The birth adds to his family life as he continues his professional football career in Türkiye.
