Report online sellers who deliver wrong items, Lagos tells consumers

2026-10-26

The Lagos State Government has urged consumers to report online sellers who deliver products that differ from what customers ordered or fail to meet the quality promised. The Lagos State Consumer Protection Agency (LASCOPA) issued the advice in a consumer awareness message on Monday, saying residents should speak up about such purchases rather than remain silent.

The agency said goods bought online should match their descriptions and orders. It encouraged affected consumers to take action when vendors fail to fulfil their obligations, identifying such cases as potential unfair business practices.

LASCOPA asked consumers to register complaints with the agency. It provided the toll-free helpline 080 0005 2726 for people seeking to report concerns. The agency also reaffirmed its commitment to consumer rights in Lagos State.
