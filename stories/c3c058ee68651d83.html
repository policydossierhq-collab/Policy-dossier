Ballon d’Or 2026: Full list of winners as rankings revealed

2026-10-26

The 2026 Ballon d’Or rankings are being unveiled during a ceremony in London on Monday, October 26. The positions announced so far include five players placed between 25th and 29th in the men’s category.

Paris Saint-Germain captain Marquinhos is ranked 25th, followed by Bayern Munich defender Dayot Upamecano in 26th. Manchester United midfielder Bruno Fernandes is 27th, Al-Qadsiah forward Julian Quiñones is 28th, and Arsenal defender William Saliba shares 29th place.

The rankings are being released as the ceremony proceeds. The supplied announcements do not yet identify the eventual winner or the players in the highest positions. England captain and Bayern Munich striker Harry Kane is among the leading contenders, alongside Barcelona’s Lamine Yamal and other leading players.

The award recognises individual performances over the relevant assessment period, considering players’ performances, achievements and overall contribution to football.
