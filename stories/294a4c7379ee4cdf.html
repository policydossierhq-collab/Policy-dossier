Atiku to campaign in all states — ADC campaign council

2026-10-26

Phrank Shaibu, director of strategic communication for the African Democratic Congress Presidential Campaign Council, says former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar will campaign in all 36 states ahead of the 2027 presidential election. He said the campaign schedule would be released soon and that no state would be excluded from Atiku’s voter engagements.

Speaking in an interview, Shaibu rejected the suggestion that the campaign would concentrate mainly on northern Nigeria. He said it would reach beyond rallies in state capitals, with town hall meetings, community engagements and visits to local government areas and villages intended to give Atiku opportunities to hear directly from Nigerians.

Shaibu framed economic hardship as a concern affecting voters across the country, arguing that it could shape political choices regardless of region. He predicted that Nigerians facing hardship would support Atiku and expressed confidence in the former vice-president’s prospects nationwide. Those remarks were Shaibu’s assessment; the campaign schedule had not yet been released.

The proposed nationwide tour contrasts with President Bola Tinubu’s stated plan to limit his personal participation in All Progressives Congress campaign rallies to 12 states. At the inauguration of the APC Presidential Campaign Council in Abuja, Tinubu said he needed to balance campaigning with the demands of governing, including the country’s financial obligations and efforts to attract investors. A campaign timetable listed rallies from Kaduna on October 24 through Lagos on January 9, 2027. The 2027 election is expected to bring campaigning by parties across the country as they seek to mobilise voters.
