Kimi Antonelli becomes youngest Formula One world champion at age 20

2026-11-01

Kimi Antonelli secured the Formula One world championship by finishing third at the Mexico Grand Prix, becoming the youngest driver to win the title. The Mercedes driver was 20 years and 68 days old when he clinched the crown.

Max Verstappen won the race, but Antonelli’s podium finish was enough to settle the championship. Antonelli’s achievement puts him more than three years ahead of Sebastian Vettel’s age when Vettel became champion in 2010.

Antonelli is also the first Italian to win the Formula One title since Alberto Ascari in 1953.
