Kidnappers abduct six in Kaduna

2026-10-27

Suspected kidnappers abducted six people during an early-morning attack on Nuru Suraj Estate in the Millennium City area of Kaduna metropolis on Tuesday. Residents were sent scrambling for safety when the assailants entered the estate and fired shots, according to accounts from the area.

A resident who identified herself as Maman Hamid said her home was close to those of the victims. She said the attackers gained access through the estate’s second gate before taking the six people to an unknown destination.

The victims’ identities and whether their families had been contacted were not clear at the time the incident was reported. The attack has added to concerns among residents about security in Millennium City, where suspected kidnappers reportedly abducted several people in another incident a few weeks earlier.

The Kaduna State Police Command had not responded to requests for comment by the time of reporting.
