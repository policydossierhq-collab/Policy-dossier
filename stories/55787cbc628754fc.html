‘Filled with content creators’ — Bwala disparages NDC campaign council list

2026-10-25

Daniel Bwala, a special adviser to President Bola Tinubu, has publicly criticized the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) for its recently unveiled National Campaign Council for the upcoming 2027 elections. He described the council as being 'filled with content creators' and alleged that the NDC is hiding the identities of its media surrogates within mainstream media.

Bwala made these remarks on social media shortly after the NDC announced its campaign council, which aims to coordinate campaign activities and mobilize support for the party's candidates in the lead-up to the elections. The NDC has designated Senator Henry Seriake Dickson as the chairman of the council, with Peter Obi, the party's presidential candidate, serving as co-chairman. Other notable appointments include Senator Cleopas Moses Zuwoghe as vice-chairman and Senator Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso as co-vice-chairman, alongside Barrister Ikenna Morgan Enekweizu as council secretary and Mallam Ibrahim Hussain Abdulkarim as director-general.

The NDC has outlined its intentions to provide strategic leadership and promote a campaign that is centered on the people and issues rather than personality. They aim to foster unity among their members and supporters as the 2027 elections approach. Bwala's comments, made during a period of heightened political activity, reflect ongoing competition and scrutiny among the nation's political parties as they finalize their operational frameworks for the elections.
