Real Madrid beat Barcelona 2-1 in La Liga Clasico

2026-10-25

In a closely contested La Liga Clasico on October 25, 2026, Real Madrid emerged victorious against Barcelona with a score of 2-1 at Camp Nou. Kylian Mbappe was the star of the match, scoring both goals for the visitors, while Raphinha found the net for Barcelona, who suffered their first domestic defeat of the season.

The match commenced with a cautious opening period, where both teams struggled to create clear scoring opportunities. Real Madrid made adjustments after losing their winger Vinicius Junior to suspension and fielded goalkeeper Andriy Lunin, who stepped in for the ill Thibaut Courtois. Despite Barcelona’s efforts, including chances from new signing Anthony Gordon, it was Mbappe who opened the scoring just before half-time. Taking advantage of an error from Barcelona defender Gerard Martin, he deflected a cross into the net, marking his tenth goal of the league season.

In the second half, Barcelona equalized shortly after the hour mark, with Raphinha scoring through the legs of Jude Bellingham after a well-timed pass from Pedri. This goal seemed to spark a resurgence in Barcelona’s attacking momentum, with players like Lamine Yamal and Martin making threatening runs towards the Real Madrid goal. However, Lunin kept Real Madrid in the game with crucial saves, particularly against Martin's header from a corner.

As the match progressed, it appeared that Barcelona might take control, but a decisive moment came late in the match. Substitute Gabriel Jesus lost the ball, allowing Real Madrid to counter quickly. Eduardo Camavinga's assist to Mbappe resulted in the Frenchman scoring his second goal into an empty net, sealing the victory for Los Blancos. This win marks Real Madrid's fifth consecutive victory across all competitions, while Barcelona, who had previously won their first nine league matches, is now facing a challenging period following two consecutive losses, including their Champions League defeat to Paris Saint-Germain.

This Clasico result has significant implications for La Liga standings, as Real Madrid now closes the gap on league leaders Barcelona, making this match one of great importance in the title race.
