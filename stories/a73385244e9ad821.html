Como Record Stunning 9-1 Win Against Lens In Champions League

2026-11-04

Como recorded a 9-1 away victory over Lens in France on Wednesday, matching the record for the most goals scored by one team in a Champions League game in the competition’s modern era. The result also marked the heaviest defeat suffered by a French team in the tournament.

Anastasios Douvikas scored three times to lead Como’s attack. The Italian side is coached by Cesc Fabregas and has won three of its four matches in this season’s league phase.

Bayern Munich also scored nine goals in a Champions League match, beating Dinamo Zagreb 9-2 in 2024. Liverpool and Real Madrid have each recorded 8-0 group-stage victories.
