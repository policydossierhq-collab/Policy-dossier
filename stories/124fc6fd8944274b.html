VIDEO: Oluremi Tinubu cheers Flamingos during World Cup final

2026-11-07

Nigeria’s First Lady, Senator Oluremi Tinubu, watched the Flamingos’ FIFA U-17 Women’s World Cup final against Japan from her residence, cheering the team as it competed for the title. A video shared on social media showed her applauding the players and chanting, “Nigeria! Nigeria!” during the match.

The Flamingos won 2-1 at the Mohammed VI Football Complex. Awawu Bashiru opened the scoring in the 39th minute, and Oluebube Umejiaku added Nigeria’s second in the 47th. Japan reduced the deficit through Fuki Ito in the 80th minute, but Nigeria held on to secure the victory.

The win made Nigeria the first African nation to claim the FIFA U-17 Women’s World Cup. It also made Nigeria the first African country to win the FIFA U-17 World Cup in both the men’s and women’s categories.

The Flamingos’ route to the title included a 2-1 defeat to Poland in their opening group match. They went on to become the first team in the competition’s history to lose its opening game and still win the tournament.
