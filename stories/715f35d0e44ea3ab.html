Gombe awards seven road contracts covering 97km

2026-11-23

Gombe State has signed agreements with construction companies for seven road projects covering approximately 97 kilometres across Balanga, Kaltungo, Shongom and Yamaltu-Deba local government areas. The state says the roads are intended to connect rural communities, ease the movement of agricultural produce and improve access to markets and other economic opportunities.

The contracts were signed at a ceremony organised by the Ministry of Works and Infrastructure. Commissioner Shehu Yerima Abdullahi said the projects form part of Governor Muhammadu Inuwa Yahaya’s infrastructure programme, which aims to extend development beyond urban centres and support rural communities. He instructed contractors to follow approved engineering specifications, quality standards and contract terms, and said the ministry would monitor work to ensure delivery within agreed timelines.

The projects are the 18.9-kilometre Talasse-Bangu-Boka Bundi-Walalunguda Road, including a spur to Dalawaja; Phase One of the four-kilometre Degri-Sikam-Nyuwar Kindiyo Road; and the 38-kilometre Lapan-Labariya-Lasanjan-Latatar-Lakunturum Road, with a spur to Burak. The package also includes the 15-kilometre Bangunji-Dadiya-Yelwa-Gombe/Yola Road, the nine-kilometre Kurjale-Pata Road, the 10.5-kilometre Dogon Ruwa-Kaltin Road and the 1.5-kilometre Kunuwal Road Network.

Permanent Secretary Danladi Adamu described the signing as a step towards implementing and supervising the projects. Representatives of EEC International Limited, SWAS GRADE Limited and DATUM Construction Nigeria Limited said their companies would meet the approved specifications and contractual requirements and complete the work within the agreed periods. The State Executive Council approved the seven projects at its most recent meeting, chaired by Governor Yahaya. Once completed, they are expected to reduce travel difficulties, improve rural-urban connections and support the movement of farm produce to markets.
