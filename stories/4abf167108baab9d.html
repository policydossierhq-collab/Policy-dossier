Oando announces final terms of N203.8 billion rights issue

Oando Plc plans to raise N203.8 billion by offering about 4.1 billion ordinary shares to existing shareholders. The shares, with a nominal value of 50 kobo each, will be offered at N50 apiece on a one-for-two basis. The company said it has secured the regulatory approvals needed to proceed.

In a regulatory filing, Oando linked the capital raise to its acquisition of Nigerian Agip Oil Company from Eni in August 2024, in a transaction valued at $783 million. The acquisition increased Oando’s participating interest in OMLs 60 to 63 from 20 per cent to 40 per cent and transferred operatorship of the assets to the company. Oando said the deal expanded its upstream operations and created opportunities to invest in production growth and cash generation.

The company expects the proceeds to strengthen its balance sheet, improve working capital and support the development of new oil and gas wells across its core upstream assets. Chief Executive Wale Tinubu said Oando’s focus was to turn the expanded asset base into sustained production growth, stronger cash generation and long-term shareholder value. He said a stronger capital structure would help fund operations and investment and provide financial flexibility.

Oando said it has approvals from Nigeria’s Securities and Exchange Commission and Nigerian Exchange, as well as the Johannesburg Stock Exchange and the Financial Surveillance Department of the South African Reserve Bank. Eligible shareholders on the Nigerian register are those listed at the close of business on 13 February 2026; the qualifying date for shareholders on the South African register is 4 December 2026. The acceptance period is scheduled to open on 7 December 2026 and close on 15 January 2027. Shareholders on the South African register will pay R0.632487 per rights share.
