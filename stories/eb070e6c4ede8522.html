[UPDATED] Ebola: Nigeria Has 54 Primary Contacts Now, Many Are In Quarantine, Says NCDC

2026-10-26

Nigeria has identified 54 primary contacts linked to its confirmed Ebola case, after tracing an e-hailing driver who transported the patient to a hospital, the Nigeria Centre for Disease Control and Prevention (NCDC) said. The agency had previously reported 53 contacts. Its Director-General, Jide Idris, said the additional contact was identified after officials received new information about the patient’s journey.

Idris said about 40 of the contacts were in quarantine, while six people assessed as being at lower risk were self-quarantining at home. They are being contacted and having their temperatures checked in the morning and evening. Three people who had changes in their health and were tested again were negative, he said. The NCDC is continuing to identify secondary contacts and says monitoring must be completed before it can determine that the outbreak risk has passed.

The Federal Government announced Nigeria’s first confirmed case of the Bundibugyo strain on October 20, 2026. The patient was a woman who had travelled to Uganda and returned to Nigeria with symptoms on October 7. The Ugandan authorities denied reports of an Ebola outbreak in the country. The health minister said the patient had been flown to Switzerland in a stable and conscious condition and had clinically improved. Idris said the Swiss government arranged the air ambulance following a diplomatic request, and rejected the suggestion that the transfer reflected a lack of confidence in Nigeria’s health system. He said the country had infectious-disease specialists and had brought in Nigerians with experience treating Ebola cases in 2014.

Idris said anyone experiencing symptoms, or concerned about someone who is ill, should contact a public health centre or call the NCDC’s toll-free hotline at 6232, which he said is staffed around the clock. Nigeria has no active positive case, but the agency is continuing to follow contacts and conduct testing.
