Lagos gives residents December 31 deadline to get health insurance

2026-10-28

Lagos State has set December 31, 2026, as the deadline for residents to obtain health insurance, with enforcement of mandatory coverage due to begin the following day. Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu announced the timeline while launching enforcement under Executive Order EO/BOS/01 of 2024 at an event in Victoria Island attended by government officials and health-sector stakeholders.

Sanwo-Olu also unveiled the Lagos Private Health Plans, intended to extend insurance to salaried workers and corporate organisations. The plans will complement Ilera Eko, which serves vulnerable residents and people working in the informal sector. The governor said the policy requires residents, employers and workers to have social health insurance, and places responsibilities on individuals, businesses and government.

He urged employers to treat workers’ healthcare as a necessary business cost, comparable to pension obligations. Sanwo-Olu also said health providers should stabilise patients needing emergency care before addressing payment or insurance status, and should not refuse emergency treatment because of financial constraints. He said 21 health maintenance organisations had endorsed participation in the new plans, which bring government and private operators together to expand coverage.

Lagos Commissioner for Health Akin Abayomi said newer medical technologies, including artificial intelligence, robotic science and modern diagnostic equipment, had raised the cost of healthcare. He argued that insurance could help shield households from rising expenses and reduce reliance on out-of-pocket payments. Adebayo Adedewe, chairman of the Lagos State Health Management Agency, said the state had increased its Health Equity Fund from N1 billion to N3 billion, with the aim of covering more than 500,000 vulnerable residents unable to afford premiums. He said the agency would continue enrolment and public-awareness efforts through community outreach and media campaigns.
