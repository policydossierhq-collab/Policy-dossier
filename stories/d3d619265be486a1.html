Court adjourns divorce case of Gov Yusuf, wife to Dec 15

2026-11-18

An Upper Area Court in Abuja’s Gudu District has adjourned the divorce case involving Kano State Governor Abba Yusuf and his estranged wife, Zainab, until December 15. Judge Ado Muktar deferred the hearing to allow the parties to file and exchange the required court documents.

Sanusi Bature, Kano State’s commissioner for information, appeared for the governor, while Zainab was represented by her lawyer, Aliyu Baba. Bature told the court he had received the legal documents on Yusuf’s behalf and requested more time to brief the governor and his legal team. Zainab did not oppose the adjournment.

After the hearing, Bature said he had learned of the case through reports that a matter involving the governor had been filed in court. He urged the public to exercise restraint, describing the dispute as a personal family matter that is before the court.

Zainab, Yusuf’s second wife, is seeking dissolution of the marriage over what the filing describes as “irreconcilable differences.” The marriage produced six children, and she has offered to refund the dowry paid by the governor. Yusuf confirmed that he was experiencing marital issues while addressing pensioners at the Government House in Kano on Monday.
