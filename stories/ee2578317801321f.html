Bayelsa boat tragedy: NEMA deploys technical team as death toll rises

2026-11-22

The National Emergency Management Agency has deployed a technical team to Bayelsa State to reinforce search and rescue operations after a boat capsized in Southern Ijaw Local Government Area. Twenty-four people have died, while 24 survivors have been rescued. The search was still under way when the agency announced the figures on Saturday.

The accident occurred on Friday, November 20, 2026, at a river junction between Oweikorogha and Otuan communities. The boat was carrying passengers from Ayama to Ukubie for a burial ceremony when it capsized.

Of those rescued, 19 were taken to Ayama Clinic. Five others, who sustained injuries of varying severity, were transferred to Family Care Hospital in Yenagoa for treatment.

NEMA said its South-South Zonal Directorate had been activated earlier, and an emergency response team had been mobilised from its Port Harcourt Operations Office. The agency is coordinating with the Bayelsa State Emergency Management Agency, the Nigerian Red Cross Society, the Nigeria Police Force, the Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps, local divers and other responders.

Governor Douye Diri visited the scene, expressed grief over the deaths and offered condolences to the victims’ families. He also commended responders for their work and continued efforts to locate possible survivors in difficult riverine terrain. NEMA said it would continue working with relevant authorities to coordinate the operation.
