Plateau assures corps members of safety, donates N6m at orientation ceremony

2026-11-07

Plateau State Governor Caleb Mutfwang has assured National Youth Service Corps members serving in the state that his administration will work with security agencies to protect them and support their welfare. He gave the assurance at the opening and swearing-in ceremony for the 2026 Batch C Stream II orientation course on Saturday at the NYSC Temporary Orientation Camp in Doi-Du, Jos South Local Government Area.

Represented at the ceremony by Youth and Sports Development Commissioner Miaphen, who chairs the state’s NYSC Governing Board, Mutfwang said the protection of corps members remained a priority. He said the state government was working with security agencies and other stakeholders to strengthen security, and urged corps members to stay alert and follow security guidelines during their service year.

The governor also called on the new corps members to contribute to national development through community service and to promote unity by respecting Nigeria’s ethnic, religious and cultural diversity, as well as the customs of their host communities. He donated N5m to welcome them to Plateau State and support the orientation exercise.

Miaphen separately donated N1m on behalf of the Ministry of Youth and Sports Development, recognising the NYSC Plateau State Dance Troupe’s performance at the ceremony. State NYSC Coordinator Jonathan Yavala urged corps members to uphold the dignity of the NYSC uniform and show discipline, tolerance, humility, respect and patriotism. He also encouraged active participation in the orientation course, relationship-building across cultural boundaries and the use of their skills and experience to address challenges in their host communities.
