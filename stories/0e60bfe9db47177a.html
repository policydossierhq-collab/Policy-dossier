OAU announces resumption date for 2026/2027 session

2026-10-26

Obafemi Awolowo University (OAU) has announced the schedule for the start of its 2026/2027 academic session. Newly admitted students are expected to arrive on campus on Sunday, November 1, while online registration is scheduled to open the following day, according to the university’s management.

Orientation for new students will run from November 2 to 6. The programme is intended to introduce them to the university’s academic environment, rules, traditions and expectations, and help prepare them for university life.

The university has also scheduled ICT training for the new students from November 9 to 13. The training will cover essential digital skills and familiarise students with the ICT facilities and platforms used to support their academic activities.

Returning undergraduates are expected back on campus on Sunday, November 15. Lectures are due to begin on Monday, November 16. Management urged new and returning students to follow the schedule and asked parents, guardians and other stakeholders to support the start of the session. Vice-Chancellor Simeon Bamire wished students a safe return and a productive academic year.
