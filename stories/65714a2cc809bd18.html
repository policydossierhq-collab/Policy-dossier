Okonjo-Iweala loses mother, as Tinubu mourns

2026-10-25

President Bola Tinubu has extended condolences to the Okonjo royal family of Ogwashi-Uku, Delta State, following the death of Prof. Kamene Okonjo, aged 96. He also expressed sympathy to her daughter, Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala, director-general of the World Trade Organization, other relatives and members of the community.

Tinubu described Prof. Okonjo as an accomplished academic whose life was marked by intellectual distinction and service. He recognised her contributions to scholarship and her commitment to advancing women and society.

The president also praised her dedication to family and community, and her role in preserving traditional institutions. He offered prayers for her eternal rest and for comfort for her family and others mourning her death.
