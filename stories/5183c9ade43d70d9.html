INEC omission boosted NDC’s support base, popularity — Gwarzo’s spokesman

2026-10-30

Ibrahim Garba Shuaibu, spokesman for Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) Kano State governorship candidate Aminu Gwarzo, says the controversy over the candidate’s name being omitted from an Independent National Electoral Commission list has increased the party’s popularity ahead of the 2027 election. INEC later restored Gwarzo’s name, prompting Shuaibu’s response on Friday.

Shuaibu said the party had not been alarmed by the omission because it believed it had met the electoral requirements. He said NDC members viewed the episode as a temporary setback that would be corrected, pointing to the steps taken from party membership through nomination and submission of the candidate’s details.

He argued that the controversy had generated public sympathy and brought more supporters to the party. Shuaibu said an event that might have been intended as a setback had instead helped strengthen the NDC’s support and encouraged more people to identify with it. These were the spokesman’s claims about the impact of the omission and restoration.

Shuaibu said the party would continue mobilising voters and promoting its programmes across Kano State. He expressed confidence that the episode had improved its prospects in the forthcoming governorship election.
