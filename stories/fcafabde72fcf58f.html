Rivers 2027: Dumo appoints Wike’s ex-commissioner campaign strategist

2026-11-20

Rivers State governorship candidate Dumo Lulu-Briggs has appointed Dr Austin Tam-George as Director of Strategy and Public Policy for his campaign council ahead of the 2027 election. The appointment was announced in a statement issued in Port Harcourt on Friday by the council’s media and publicity directorate.

The council said the appointment reflected its focus on strategic planning, policy development, implementation and public engagement. It said Tam-George would coordinate the campaign’s strategy, public policy framework and communications work.

Tam-George previously served as Rivers State Commissioner for Information and Communications under former Governor Nyesom Wike. The council said his experience also spans academia, government, international development, corporate affairs and strategic advisory services. He has taught at the University of Cape Town and Pan-Atlantic University, and has engaged with UNESCO and the New Partnership for Africa’s Development on areas including governance, education, youth development and institutional capacity building.

His academic and executive education includes a PhD from the University of the Witwatersrand in South Africa, the Senior Executive Fellows programme at Harvard University’s John F. Kennedy School of Government, a postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Cape Town and executive education at IESE Business School in Spain. The statement also identified him as a member of the Harvard Business Review Advisory Council.

Lulu-Briggs said he was committed to bringing accomplished professionals and experienced people into his team to contribute to changes in governance and development in Rivers State. He welcomed Tam-George as the campaign continued discussions about its agenda for the state.
