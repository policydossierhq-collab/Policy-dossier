UNIUYO To Graduate 22,961 Students, 214 With First Class Honours

2026-11-10

The University of Uyo plans to graduate 22,961 students at its 31st and 32nd combined convocation ceremonies on Saturday, November 14, 2026. The event is scheduled to take place at the university’s permanent site in Nwaniba, Akwa Ibom State. Vice-Chancellor Professor Christopher Nyong Ekong announced the figures at a media briefing ahead of the ceremony.

The graduating students include 19,217 receiving first degrees, 814 diploma recipients, 248 certificate recipients and 2,682 postgraduate graduands. Among those receiving first degrees, 214 will graduate with First Class Honours. A further 7,216 earned Second Class Upper, 10,468 Second Class Lower, 917 Third Class and 402 Pass degrees.

The university will also award honorary doctorate degrees to four Nigerians: Taraba State Governor Dr Agbu Kefas; Niger Delta Development Commission Managing Director Chief Samuel Ogbuku; Tantita Security Services Nigeria Limited Chairman High Chief Government Oweizide Ekpemupolo, known as Tompolo; and Ubotex Nigeria Limited Managing Director Mr Ubong Obot. Ekong said the awards recognise their contributions to human and societal development in their respective fields.

The total of 22,961 is 10,511 higher than the 12,450 graduands at the previous combined convocation, held in November 2024—an increase of about 84.4 per cent. The number of First Class graduates is also up by 40 from the 174 recorded at that ceremony. Ekong said this would be the final time his administration combines multiple graduating sessions, adding that it intends to restore regular convocation schedules by processing and approving examination results promptly.
