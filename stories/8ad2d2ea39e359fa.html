Ethiopia receives Russian fighter jets, air defence systems

2026-10-29

Ethiopia has received 12 Russian Su-35 fighter jets and expects four S-400 air-defence systems, Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed said. The deliveries are intended to strengthen the country’s military capabilities as fighting intensifies in the northern Tigray region. The S-400 systems are expected but have not been reported as delivered.

Abiy said the aircraft were supplied under an earlier agreement with the Russian government and arrived a year ahead of schedule. He described the delivery as a milestone in efforts to strengthen national security, and said the jets would support Ethiopia’s sovereignty as well as regional security and stability. He also said Ethiopia had become the first country in sub-Saharan Africa to possess this type of advanced combat technology.

The announcement coincided with the start of a two-day Russia-Africa summit in Moscow, which Abiy is attending. The gathering is described as an effort by Russian President Vladimir Putin to expand Russia’s presence in Africa. Abiy has called Putin a close friend of Ethiopia.

The military deliveries come amid renewed clashes between Ethiopian government forces and the Tigray People’s Liberation Front. TPLF forces had taken control of Mekelle and Alula Aba Nega Airport, but government and allied forces retook Mekelle on Sunday after the group said it had temporarily left the regional capital to operate elsewhere. Eritrean troops crossed into Tigray in early October, adding to tensions and concerns about a wider regional conflict.
