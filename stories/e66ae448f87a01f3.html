Real Madrid’s Courtois Out Of Clasico With Flu

2026-10-25
Image: Channels TV

Real Madrid goalkeeper Thibaut Courtois will miss Sunday’s Clasico against Barcelona after falling ill with flu, the club announced a few hours before the match. Madrid described him as a last-minute withdrawal from the squad.

Ukrainian goalkeeper Andriy Lunin is set to replace Courtois against Barcelona, who are unbeaten under coach Hansi Flick. The match is at Camp Nou.

The absence adds to Madrid’s personnel problems for the game: winger Vinicius Junior is also unavailable because of suspension. Madrid are third in the league, six points behind leaders Barcelona, making the meeting significant to the title race.
