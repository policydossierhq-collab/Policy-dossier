APC chair attacks Atiku over lack of projects in Plateau as VP

2026-10-28

All Progressives Congress National Chairman Nentawe Yilwatda challenged former vice-president Atiku Abubakar to identify a major project worth at least N10 billion that was completed in Plateau State during Abubakar’s eight years in office. Yilwatda made the remarks on Wednesday at the PBAT Door-to-Door Movement in Jos, where he was seeking support for President Bola Tinubu ahead of the 2027 general election.

Yilwatda said he could not recall a major road or other large-scale project delivered in Plateau during that period. He contrasted that record, as he described it, with what he said Tinubu’s administration was bringing to the state. He cited a university and the Federal Medical Centre among the projects associated with the current administration, and said it had addressed issues in healthcare, education, infrastructure and security.

The APC chair identified insecurity as the main concern for Plateau residents and credited Tinubu with addressing it. His comments were made while campaigning for the president’s re-election; the source material did not provide further detail about the projects he named or his assessment of security in the state.

Plateau State Governor Caleb Mutfwang also urged residents to support Tinubu’s re-election. He argued that the president should be allowed to complete an eight-year term, referring to a North-South power-sharing arrangement. Mutfwang said former president Muhammadu Buhari had served eight years representing the North and that the South should likewise be allowed eight years. He also asked Nigerians to be patient with Tinubu’s economic policies, saying they needed time to produce results.
