US Air Force captain arrested in Japan

2026-11-14

Japanese police arrested a 27-year-old US Air Force captain on suspicion of drunk driving in Okinawa on Saturday. A prefectural police spokesman said the man was driving in Okinawa City at about 3:09 a.m. The captain was assigned to Kadena Air Base, according to a report by Kyodo News.

Police said it was unclear whether the captain had violated a curfew imposed by US officials. The base did not immediately provide comment.

The arrest came a week after Japan and the United States agreed on measures to prevent crimes by US service members in Okinawa. The agreement followed public anger over a murder allegedly involving an American Marine.

About 54,000 US military personnel are stationed in Japan, most of them in Okinawa. US officials had also announced an extension of a curfew for lower-ranking service members through December 7 and a ban on all service members drinking alcohol outside their homes between midnight and 5 a.m.
