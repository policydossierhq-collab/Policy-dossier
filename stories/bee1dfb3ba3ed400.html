Security agencies rescue eight kidnap victims in Kaduna

2026-11-03

Eight people, including an eight-year-old girl, have been rescued in coordinated security operations in Kaduna State, according to Commissioner of Police Muhammad Rabiu. The operations took place from October 31 to November 2 in Millennium City and Marabar Rido, both in Chikun Local Government Area, with the Kaduna State Government’s cooperation.

The rescued people were identified as Jamila Muhammad, 27; Talatu Yakubu, 40; Yunana Bawa, 30; Calista Bishop, 27; Chidinma Onuwere, 36; Eucharia Obi, 41; Godiya Victor, 50; and Miracle Alex, eight. Rabiu said the intelligence-led operations were part of continuing efforts to identify and disrupt networks responsible for repeated kidnappings in the area.

The rescue followed the release of Ahmad Zakka and members of his family from captivity on October 15. Rabiu said the successive operations reflected security agencies’ resolve to free abducted people and dismantle criminal networks. He credited the Office of the National Security Adviser with coordination, and cited operational support from the Police, intelligence from the Department of State Services and the Military’s involvement.

Rabiu said agencies were intensifying operations across Kaduna State and planned to deploy additional personnel and equipment, including 30 operational vans and eight armoured personnel carriers. He noted that difficult terrain in parts of the state can help criminal groups carry out attacks and evade security forces.

The commissioner urged residents, traditional rulers and community leaders to share credible information about suspected kidnappers and their activities, assuring the public that information would be treated confidentially. He also called on media organisations to avoid unverified reporting that could compromise operations or cause public alarm. Rabiu said the agencies and state government would continue their efforts to restore peace across the state.
