Police nab masquerades for extorting residents in A’Ibom

2026-10-29

Police in Akwa Ibom State have arrested two Ekpo masquerade participants over allegations of extorting and assaulting residents in Ikot Ekpene. The command said the suspects were detained at about 6 p.m. on Monday, October 26, along the Ukana/Abak highway during an operational show of force. The arrest was announced in a statement released by police spokesperson Timfom John on Thursday.

The suspects were identified as Ine Isong, 29, of Ukana Ikot Esoh Village, and Emmanuel Ekpenyong, 20, of Ikono Local Government Area. Police said they were allegedly found with Ekpo masquerade costumes and attire, as well as machetes, spears and arrows.

According to preliminary reports cited by police, the suspects and other people allegedly involved had been terrorising residents and demanding money from members of the public. The statement also said some people, particularly women, were reportedly assaulted. Police said investigations were continuing to establish what happened and identify others who may be connected to the alleged offences.

Commissioner of Police Baba Azare warned against using cultural practices or masquerade activities to intimidate, extort or harm people. He said the command respected lawful cultural traditions but would act against individuals or groups that took the law into their own hands or threatened residents’ safety.
