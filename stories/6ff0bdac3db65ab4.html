NANS hails FG’s move to manufacture passports

2026-11-26

The National Association of Nigerian Students (NANS) has welcomed the establishment of a National Passport Manufacturing Centre, describing the move as a step towards reducing foreign dependence and strengthening Nigeria’s sovereignty. The association also praised Interior Minister Olubunmi Tunji-Ojo for leading the initiative and for reforms to the passport system.

NANS President Akinteye Babatunde Afeez made the remarks in a statement issued on Thursday. He called the centre a historic achievement, saying Nigeria had not produced its own international passports in the more than six decades since independence. The association described the centre’s groundbreaking ceremony as a significant milestone for national identity and public service delivery.

The students’ body said reforms to passport applications and renewals had eased delays and bureaucratic difficulties faced by Nigerians. It said the changes had benefited students studying abroad and other Nigerians living outside the country by making it easier to obtain or renew travel documents. These assessments were made by the association in its statement.

NANS also praised Tunji-Ojo’s leadership at the Ministry of Interior, saying it had broadened perceptions of the ministry’s work. The association credited him with changing the status quo in passport services and commended President Bola Tinubu for appointing him to the role.

The association said it would continue to recognise policies and initiatives that improve people’s lives, expand opportunities and strengthen Nigeria’s sovereignty. It described the passport manufacturing centre as the latest in a series of reforms introduced under the minister.
