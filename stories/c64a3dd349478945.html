‘I will forever miss you’ – Toluwanisings’ wife breaks down in tears at night of tributes

2026-10-27

Akanbi Elizabeth broke down in tears as she remembered her late husband, gospel singer Olayemi “Toluwanisings” Bolaji, at a night of tributes in Ibadan, Oyo State, on Tuesday. Addressing mourners at Hall of Grace, Jogor Centre, she spoke of the pain of losing him and asked him not to forget her and their children. “Toluwanisings, my dear husband. I will forever miss you. Please do not forget your children and me,” she said.

Relatives, friends, colleagues and associates gathered to honour the singer, known for the song “Kabiyesi Olorun Ayo.” Gospel artistes and actors, including Adeyinka Alaseyori, Esther Igbekele, Laolu Gbenjo, Akintayo Akinwande, Bidemi Olaoba, Peterson Okopi, Sarah Apekeola and Bimbo Oshin, attended. Tributes included worship, songs and personal reflections on Bolaji’s life and musical legacy.

Bolaji and Elizabeth married in September 2021 and had two daughters. He died on 15 October after slumping at his residence in Ibadan. The supplied information does not specify the year of his death.

His burial arrangements have become a point of dispute within the family, which has not settled whether he should be buried in a cemetery, at his residence or in his hometown. Relatives say arrangements should remain on hold until authorities establish the circumstances of his death. They have called for a transparent and impartial investigation and said they would continue paying mortuary costs while inquiries proceed. The family has also alleged possible foul play, citing what it described as an absence of visible mourning at the residence when seven relatives visited; that claim has not been independently established in the information provided.
