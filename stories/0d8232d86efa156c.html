Ernest Shonekan Centre Launches Portal to Track Legislation Affecting Nigeria’s Business Environment

2026-10-27

The Ernest Shonekan Centre for Legislative Reforms and Economic Development has launched a digital portal for tracking proposed laws with potential implications for Nigeria’s business environment. The centre is an initiative of the Nigerian Economic Summit Group (NESG). The Business Bill Intelligence Portal was launched at the 32nd Nigerian Economic Summit, held at the Transcorp Hilton Hotel in Abuja under the theme “Growth that Works: Delivering Jobs, Productivity and Shared Prosperity.”

Users can browse and search bills, and apply available filters to find legislation of interest. An analytics dashboard summarises bills and shows their distribution across legislative stages. Individual bill pages provide each proposal’s status, latest recorded action and legislative history, allowing users to follow its progress.

The centre says the portal is intended to make it easier for businesses and other stakeholders to identify relevant proposals and understand their possible effects. Laws can shape investment decisions, business operations, market access, regulatory requirements and the cost of doing business, but tracking bills and determining their relevance to particular sectors can be difficult. ESC Chairman Kyari Bukar said the initiative aims to encourage informed participation in legislative reform and support laws that improve the business environment while protecting the public interest.

The portal builds on the NESG’s work with the legislature. In 2016, the group established the National Assembly Business Environment Roundtable to bring legislative and private-sector participants together on reform; it later supported the State Assemblies Business Environment Roundtable, extending dialogue to the subnational level. Established in 2023, ESC focuses on research, advocacy and engagement on legislative reform and economic development.

ESC Executive Director Uchenna Ogbonna said the portal is intended for a broad range of users, including businesses, citizens, civil society organisations, journalists, researchers, government agencies and legislators. The centre says it aims to support awareness, informed participation and dialogue on legislation affecting Nigeria’s economy. The portal is available at https://billtracker.shonekancentre.org.
