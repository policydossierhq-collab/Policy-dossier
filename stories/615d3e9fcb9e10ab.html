Obi to publish Anambra achievements in 10-day series

2026-11-16

Peter Obi, the Nigeria Democratic Congress’s 2027 presidential candidate, says he will publish a 10-day series detailing his record as governor of Anambra State. Titled “Record of Service,” it is set to begin on Tuesday and will describe his interventions, achievements and responsibilities during his tenure.

Obi announced the plan in a post on X on Monday, shortly after arriving in the United States for engagements in Washington, DC. He said he was making his record public in response to repeated scrutiny of his past and would present some of the accomplishments from his time as governor.

He addressed allegations that he abandoned Anambra during an erosion crisis, saying he had not avoided responsibilities entrusted to him. The accusations were also raised by President Bola Tinubu at an APC rally in Akwa Ibom on November 6, where Tinubu criticised Obi’s handling of erosion in the state and contrasted it with efforts to confront the Atlantic Ocean in Lagos.

Obi said his campaigns had focused on policies, ideas and practical solutions rather than personal criticism. He said he had given his best in each role he had held, while acknowledging that not every intended result could be achieved. He also said he would continue updating supporters on his engagements and other developments as campaign activity gathered momentum.
