Relegation Panic: Abia dissolves Enyimba management, technical crew

2026-11-17

The Abia State Government has dissolved the management structure and technical crew of Enyimba International Football Club with immediate effect, as the club struggles near the bottom of the Nigeria Premier Football League. Enyimba are 18th after 13 matches in the 2026/27 season, with three wins, two draws and eight defeats. Their latest loss was a 1-0 home defeat to Ikorodu City on Sunday, leaving them on 11 points.

In a statement signed by Commissioner for Information Okey Kanu, the government said it had identified administrative and structural problems contributing to the club’s poor performance. It said broad intervention was needed to address issues affecting Enyimba’s results, administration and operations.

The Ministry of Sports and Youth Development has been directed to lead the restructuring, review the club’s management framework and carry out administrative, technical, operational and institutional reforms. Management and technical personnel affected by the decision, along with anyone holding club assets, property, official documents or equipment, have been told to hand them over to the ministry. Further information on the handover and administrative process is expected from the ministry. People within the club say former Super Eagles captain Nwankwo Kanu may remain chairman, although the government’s announcement did not confirm his position.

The intervention follows a period of instability at the club, including frequent coaching changes. Enyimba had four coaches across 29 league matches in the previous campaign. Concerns have also been raised about recruitment, player welfare and administrative continuity. The club’s most recent league title came in the 2023/24 season under Finidi George. Enyimba have won the CAF Champions League twice, in 2003 and 2004. The state government described the club as part of Abia’s sporting heritage and said the restructuring aims to strengthen its institutions, improve accountability and restore its competitive standing. Its immediate task is to move clear of the relegation zone.
