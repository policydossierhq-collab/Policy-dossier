EFCC recovers additional $40 million from Nestoil, total hits $100 million

2026-10-27

The Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) has recovered an additional $40 million from Nestoil Limited, bringing total payments under a repayment arrangement to $100 million. The lenders said the latest payment was made on October 19, 2026, following an earlier $60 million payment reported in August.

The arrangement was reached at a meeting chaired by EFCC Chairman Olanipekun Olukoyede to address obligations involving Nestoil, its affiliate Neconde Energy Limited and a consortium of lenders. It sets out a framework for orderly repayment, subject to the terms agreed by the parties and applicable laws. The lenders described the latest recovery as an encouraging development, but said a substantial portion of the debt remains unpaid.

The consortium has reaffirmed its commitment to working with the EFCC and other relevant stakeholders to recover the outstanding amounts. Its members include Access Bank, Zenith Bank, Ecobank, African Export-Import Bank, First Bank of Nigeria, First City Monument Bank, United Bank for Africa and Union Bank of Nigeria.

The lenders said in June 2026 that Nestoil’s total indebtedness had reached approximately $1.084 billion and N469.43 billion. The facilities originated as bilateral loans dating back to 2010 and were consolidated under a restructuring arrangement known as the “Global Club,” which took effect in 2023. The lenders said repayment defaults continued after the restructuring.

The dispute escalated in October 2025, when a Federal High Court in Lagos granted a Mareva injunction freezing assets, bank accounts and shares linked to Nestoil, Neconde and their promoters. In June 2026, the Supreme Court set aside interim preservative orders granted by the Court of Appeal and directed the parties to return to the lower court to address the substantive issues. The lenders said that decision did not extinguish the debt or invalidate the recovery process. They say the remaining obligations are still to be settled under the repayment plan.
