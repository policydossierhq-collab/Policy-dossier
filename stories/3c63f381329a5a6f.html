NDC Unveils Campaign Council, Names Dickson, Obi, Kwankwaso As Members

2026-10-25

The Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) has officially formed its national campaign council in anticipation of the 2027 general elections. The announcement was made on Sunday, revealing Senator Seriake Dickson as the chairman of the council. In prominent leadership positions, the NDC's presidential candidate, Peter Obi, serves as co-chairman, while the vice-presidential candidate, Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso, acts as co-vice chairman.

In addition to Dickson, other notable members of the council include Senator Cleopas Zuwoghe, who is appointed as vice chairman, and former Secretary to the Government of the Federation, Babachir Lawal, who will serve on the National Campaign Advisory Council. The campaign council includes various individuals tasked with different roles, highlighting a broad organizational strategy aimed at galvanizing support for the party's agenda.

The NDC has also outlined the formation of a 40-member national campaign advisory council and appointed 37 state campaign coordinators to streamline efforts across Nigeria. Key appointed roles include Osa Director as Director of Communication and Media, and retd. Major General John Enenche as Director of Security and Intelligence, emphasizing a commitment to a focused and secure campaign environment.

In its official communication, the NDC expressed a strong commitment to providing strategic leadership, coordinating campaign activities nationwide, and mobilizing support behind its vision for a 'new, just, and prosperous Nigeria.' The council aims to cultivate unity among party members and appeal to well-meaning Nigerians as a collective effort to address the country's challenges. This development is significant as it sets the foundation for the party's strategic approach in the upcoming elections.
