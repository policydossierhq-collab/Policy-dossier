Ebola: Abia sets up isolation centre — Otti

2026-10-30

Abia State has activated precautionary measures against Ebola and designated an isolation centre at the recently renovated Mother and Child Hospital in Umuahia. Governor Alex Otti said the facility would not be opened for general public use and would be available if needed. The state has no reported Ebola cases, he said, urging residents to follow health protocols without panicking.

Otti announced the measures during his monthly media chat for October in Umuahia. He said the state had put response protocols in place and was prepared to act if a case emerged.

The governor also reported that 31,000 people had been screened through the Abia State Eye Health Management Bureau, with 1,811 patients receiving surgery. Of those operations, 304 were free cataract surgeries. Abia has established 25 eye centres across its 17 local government areas, and Otti said he had approved four more, bringing the planned total to 29.

On public transport, Otti said Abia had chosen electric vehicles rather than compressed natural gas (CNG) buses for its system. He said the state was not opposed to CNG, and that companies had shown interest in establishing refuelling stations for other users. He also used the media briefing to review his administration’s work in areas including roads, healthcare, power and utilities.
