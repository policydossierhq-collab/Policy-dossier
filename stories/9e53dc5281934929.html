Mbah: My opponents busy shopping for shortcuts in Abuja

2026-11-10

Enugu State Governor Peter Mbah accused some potential opponents in the 2027 governorship election of seeking ways to compromise the vote instead of presenting their plans to residents. He made the remarks on Tuesday during a campaign tour of the Ogboli and Igbo-Ano Development Centres in Uzo Uwani Local Government Area, including stops in Adani and Ugbene Ajima.

Mbah said, in his view, the rivals had not presented manifestos because they lacked records and plans for the state. He alleged that they were exploring ways to manipulate voters’ choices and accused them of involvement in certificate forgery. He urged residents to reject them at the polls. The source material provided no response from the people he criticised.

The governor also said construction of the Nsukka-Adani Road and the Edem-Nrobo–Abbi-Nimbo Road would begin in earnest as the rainy season receded. He gave no further details about the timing or cost of the projects.

During the tour, Mbah visited Our Lady Health of the Sick Maternity Hospital in Adani and pledged to equip it to the standard of the state’s Type 2 Primary Healthcare Centre, including providing ultrasound equipment. He also promised a classroom block and an innovation hub for Santa Maria Nursery and Primary School, where students welcomed him.
