Full list: NDC unveils 2027 presidential campaign council

2026-10-25
Image: Vanguard

The NDC has announced a presidential campaign council for the 2027 election. The party said the body would coordinate its campaign across the country and mobilise support for its vision of a new, just and prosperous Nigeria.

The information available about the announcement does not identify council members or describe its structure, responsibilities beyond campaign coordination, or plans for carrying out its work.

The council’s stated role places it at the centre of the party’s preparations for a nationwide presidential campaign. Its task, as outlined by the party, is to organise campaigning and build support around its vision; further details about its membership and activities were not provided.
