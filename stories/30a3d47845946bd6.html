It’s dangerous to buy Christmas clothes without paying tithes – cleric

2026-10-25

Pastor David Ibiyeomie, founder of Salvation Ministries, expressed concern over the practice of tithing among Christians, particularly as the Christmas season approaches. In a recent sermon that gained attention on social media, he cautioned believers against overlooking their tithe commitments, stressing that they should not purchase festive clothing or gifts before fulfilling this religious duty.

Ibiyeomie articulated that neglecting tithes could lead to various misfortunes, which some might wrongly attribute to spiritual attacks. He recounted personal experiences involving vehicle breakdowns that he claims were linked to drivers’ failure to pay their tithes. One incident he described involved a burst tyre during a journey, which he attributed to the driver’s alleged lack of tithing. Similarly, he mentioned an instance concerning a driver of his Rolls-Royce, again citing burst tyres as proof of the consequences tied to unpaid tithes.

The pastor's message highlighted the belief that financial tribulations should not immediately be placed at the feet of the devil, promoting a more introspective approach to one’s religious obligations. He emphasized the importance of accuracy in tithe calculation and warned against casual approaches that allow individuals to contribute what they deem fit rather than what is deemed correct.

Ibiyeomie went further to implore Christians to take tithing seriously, especially during the festive season. He warned that neglecting this practice could have adverse effects on their families, hinting at potential dangers associated with not adhering to one's spiritual responsibilities. His statements challenge believers to critically assess their commitments and seek to align their actions with their faith, rather than attributing life’s challenges solely to external forces.
