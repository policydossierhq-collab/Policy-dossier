APC youth support group urges unity, warns against divisions ahead of 2027 elections

2026-10-29

The All Progressives Congress National Young Stakeholders Forum has urged members of the party’s youth wing to respect internal procedures and avoid actions that could deepen divisions ahead of the 2027 general elections. The group also expressed support for National Youth Leader Dayo Israel and called on party youths to focus on mobilising for President Bola Tinubu’s re-election.

At a press conference in Abuja on Thursday, the forum’s National Publicity Secretary, Shehu Attah, said disagreements should be handled by the appropriate party authorities. He cautioned against factional manoeuvres and efforts to establish competing centres of authority, saying such actions could cast doubt on the recognised youth leadership. The forum did not name the people or groups it said were challenging Israel’s office.

The appeal follows public scrutiny of a video Israel posted and later deleted, which showed seven new Toyota Land Cruiser Prado vehicles reportedly allocated to youth leaders for campaign activities. The vehicles prompted questions about resources for political mobilisation. Remi Tinubu had also disclosed that utility vehicles, campaign materials and footwear were being provided for APC youth leaders.

The forum passed a vote of confidence in Israel, describing his office as a link between the party leadership and young members. Attah credited him with initiatives including the APC Youth House, the Nest Resource Centre and Renewed Hope Conversations programmes at tertiary institutions. He also said more than 100 party youths and youth leaders had taken part in overseas training and capacity-building programmes over the past two years.

The group commended Israel’s role in facilitating campaign vehicles for selected youth leaders and the earlier distribution of buses to other party members. Attah said the initiatives had improved morale and addressed calls for greater recognition, while warning that factional disputes could discourage young people seeking a place in the party. The forum urged members to let recognised party structures operate as preparations for the 2027 elections advance.
