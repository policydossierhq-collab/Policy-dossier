“Advertising Brings a Player Once, Actually Paying Winners Brings Them Back” — Helabet Representative Says

2026-10-27

Helabet representative Olajay says the sportsbook is redirecting some marketing spending toward player experience, arguing that advertising can attract a first-time user but service and reliable payouts influence whether that person stays. He said the company is putting more resources into bonuses, localisation, staff and operational funds intended to support prompt payment of legitimate winnings.

Olajay linked the shift to rising marketing costs, competition and compliance requirements, as well as the naira’s decline against the US dollar. He said some customer-acquisition expenses are tied to dollar costs, making a strategy based on continually increasing advertising spending difficult to sustain. Helabet plans to introduce a 305% welcome bonus, alongside a 25% daily bonus on a first deposit, a weekly rebate and a promotional-code store where players can exchange points for single or accumulator bets. The company also offers free mobile data for MTN and Glo users, which Olajay described as a Nigeria-specific feature.

The representative said regulatory changes have added complexity to operating in Nigeria. Following the late-2024 decentralisation of gaming regulation, states gained authority over the sector. Olajay said the Federation of State Gaming Regulators of Nigeria and its Universal Reciprocity Certificate allowed Helabet to operate under a unified certificate across 22 member states, rather than seeking separate licences in each. He also said the company is adjusting to the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 and would not compromise on compliance, including working with state regulators and meeting tax obligations.

Helabet’s stated aim is to become an operator that Nigerians recommend based on its odds, event selection, bonuses and withdrawals. Olajay said the company is considering sponsoring a Nigerian football club and has drawn up a shortlist, with negotiations expected to begin later. He presented local hiring and investment in sports as part of the company’s longer-term approach, alongside its focus on player service and regulatory compliance.
