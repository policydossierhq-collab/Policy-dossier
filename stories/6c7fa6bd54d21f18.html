2027: Keyamo faults opposition over ‘rig and die’ claim

2026-11-05

Aviation and Aerospace Development Minister Festus Keyamo has criticised opposition politicians promoting the “rig and die” slogan ahead of Nigeria’s 2027 general elections. In a post on X on Thursday, he argued sarcastically that the phrase could make it harder for politicians to blame an electoral defeat on fraud if the vote was free and fair.

Keyamo said those who claimed an election had been rigged would, in effect, be challenged to show how many people they had killed on election day. He suggested that adopting the slogan would leave losing politicians unable to use allegations of rigging to explain their results.

The phrase was popularised by former Deputy Senate President Ovie Omo-Agege, who used it at a campaign event in Uvwie Local Government Area of Delta State on October 23. Omo-Agege is seeking election to represent Delta Central Senatorial District under the Nigeria Democratic Congress. At the event, he threatened that people caught manipulating the 2027 election would be killed and said there would be no recourse to the courts.

Omo-Agege’s remarks drew criticism. His media aide, Godwin Anaughe, later said the warning was aimed at people who manipulate results or steal electoral materials, not voters or innocent citizens. Omo-Agege maintained that the slogan was intended as a warning against electoral malpractice and a call to defend the people’s mandate.
