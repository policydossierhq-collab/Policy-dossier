2027: NDC campaign DG urges Atiku to join Obi-Kwankwaso

2026-10-28

Ibrahim Abdulkarim, Director-General of the Nigeria Democratic Congress (NDC) Presidential Campaign Council, has called on former Vice-President Atiku Abubakar and other opposition leaders to join the alliance backing Peter Obi and Rabiu Musa Kwankwaso ahead of the 2027 presidential election. He said opposition parties and figures needed to unite behind a common platform to improve their chances against the ruling All Progressives Congress (APC).

Abdulkarim said the coalition was taking shape within the NDC, with Obi as its presidential candidate and Kwankwaso agreeing to work with him. He argued that the alliance did not have to be formed through another party, adding that Kwankwaso had set aside his own ambition. Atiku and other opposition figures, he said, were welcome to join and bring broader national reach.

He also responded to criticism of the NDC’s campaign organisation and its relationship with support groups backing Obi. Abdulkarim rejected comparisons between the party and the Obidient movement, saying a political party should not be measured against a support group. He said more than a thousand support groups were part of the NDC.

Addressing concerns among voters that their ballots might not count, Abdulkarim said the party had developed measures to protect votes in 2027. He urged people worried about electoral integrity to support the NDC and said it would campaign peacefully across the country, including in remote areas. He added that the party intended to reassure voters before the election that their votes would be protected.
