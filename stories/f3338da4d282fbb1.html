FG begins N50,000 monthly cash relief for vulnerable Nigerian households

2026-10-30

The Federal Government has started a monthly cash relief programme providing N50,000 to eligible vulnerable households, according to Humanitarian Affairs and Poverty Reduction Minister Bernard Doro. In a statement issued in Abuja on October 30, 2026, Doro described the National Emergency Relief and Stabilisation Intervention (NERSI) as a presidential initiative intended to help households manage rising living costs, including higher food prices and the cost of essential goods.

The payments are intended to help recipients meet essential needs. The programme will be reviewed periodically to reflect changes in living costs, and beneficiaries will be notified directly before any payment adjustments. The ministry is working with licensed digital financial service providers to deliver the funds securely and transparently.

Beneficiaries are expected to receive text messages from service providers seeking consent before payments are made into bank accounts or mobile wallets. Doro said people would not need to apply, pay a fee or register through an agent. He warned against sharing Personal Identification Numbers or Bank Verification Numbers, and advised anyone unsure about a message or asked for money to call the NERSI helpline at 0700-022-2222.

Doro said NERSI would complement existing federal social protection and economic support programmes, and linked it to the government’s Renewed Hope Agenda. Separately, the World Bank reported that the Federal Government had disbursed N658.9 billion in direct cash transfers to 10.46 million poor and vulnerable households by September 2026. The report said biometric and digital verification were used to identify recipients. It also said the impact of cash transfers would depend on extending coverage, improving payment adequacy and regularity, and building a more sustainable system with dedicated federal and state budget allocations and gradually increased government funding.
