Leprosy mission partners Britain on tree planting

2026-11-03

The Leprosy Mission Nigeria (TLMN), working with a project partner from Great Britain, has planted 60 economic trees at a leprosarium in Minna, Niger State. The planting included 20 orange trees, 20 mango trees and 20 palm trees, according to the information provided by the mission.

Rev. Dr. Solomon Bityong, a TLMN project partner, said a healthy environment was essential to human survival and urged Nigerians to support similar efforts to address climate change. TLMN Minna Project Officer Habila Diko said the initiative was intended to bring environmental sustainability into the mission’s climate-smart agriculture and livelihood programmes.

Diko said the tree planting also formed part of The Leprosy Mission International’s broader commitment to promoting a clean and healthy environment. Community leader Bello Umar thanked the mission and said residents would care for and preserve the trees.

Hauwa Usman, an elderly community leader, said residents had often travelled to their farmlands to find shade during hot weather. She said the new trees could provide shade within their compounds as they grow.
