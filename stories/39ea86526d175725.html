Navy rescues Bayelsa commissioner, NSCDC official, 5 others from drowning

2026-11-09

Seven people were rescued after a passenger boat suffered a mishap in waters near Abedigbene, in Bayelsa State’s Brass Local Government Area, on Thursday. The passengers included Bayelsa Commissioner for Trade, Industry and Investment Ebieri Jones, an official of the Nigerian Security and Civil Defence Corps, and five others. A police officer was still missing, according to a Navy operational report.

The boat was travelling from Yenagoa to Akassa and reportedly had eight people aboard. After receiving a distress report from a local fisherman, personnel from the Nigerian Navy’s Forward Operating Base FORMOSO, deployed at Labrador Lagosgbene Houseboat, mobilised to the scene and began search-and-rescue operations. Local divers and members of the Abedigbene fishing community assisted.

The survivors were taken to the houseboat and given first aid. Preliminary assessments found no visible injuries, the report said. On Friday, Jones and the six other survivors were taken to Yenagoa to reunite with their families. Search efforts continued for the missing police officer.

The Navy said the operation reflected its response to emergencies and its work to protect lives and improve maritime safety in its area of responsibility. FOB FORMOSO Commanding Officer Sunday Haruna praised the personnel for their response and professionalism, and acknowledged the support of local divers and the community.
