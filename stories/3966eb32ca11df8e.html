CIBN honours 755 leaders at fellowship investiture

2026-10-30

The Chartered Institute of Bankers of Nigeria (CIBN) is set to honour 755 leaders and professionals at its 2026 Fellowship Investiture in Lagos tomorrow. The awardees come from banking, government, the private sector and academia. The Institute says the honours recognise contributions to banking, the economy, and the promotion of ethics and professionalism.

The programme will include 18 honorary fellowships, the election of 460 Associates as Fellows, and the induction of 277 senior management staff from banks and academics as Honorary Senior Members. Among those named for recognition are Lagos State Deputy Chief of Staff Sam Egube; Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation Managing Director and Chief Executive Thompson Oludare Sunday; and Bank of Sierra Leone Governor Ibrahim Stevens.

Other awardees include Famfa Oil Limited Vice Chairman Folorunsho Alakija, Federal Airports Authority of Nigeria Managing Director and Chief Executive Olubunmi Kuku, Dangote Cement Plc Chairman Emmanuel Ikazoboh, and Quest Merchant Bank Managing Director and Chief Executive Afolabi Olorode. Ogun State Secretary to the Government Tokunbo Talabi and Summit Bank Limited Managing Director and Chief Executive Siraju Salisu are also listed.

The keynote address, titled “Bank Recapitalisation: Leveraging New Bank Capital for Nigeria’s Industrial Transformation,” will be delivered by Nigerian Economic Summit Group Chief Executive Tayo Aduloju. Lagos State Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu and Minister of State for Budget and Economic Planning Doris Uzoka-Anite are expected to attend as special guests of honour.
