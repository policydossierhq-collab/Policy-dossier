2027: Obi Pledges To Jail Electoral Offenders

2026-10-28

Peter Obi, the Nigeria Democratic Congress presidential candidate, has pledged to strengthen electoral laws and impose tougher penalties on those who manipulate elections if elected president. He made the commitment at an NDC town hall meeting in Akwanga Local Government Area of Nasarawa State, where he set out proposed changes to the electoral system.

Obi said his administration would focus on strengthening political parties and improving electoral processes, with the aim of limiting manipulation and rebuilding public confidence in elections. He said electoral fraud should lead to prosecution and imprisonment for Independent National Electoral Commission officials and others found responsible for altering results.

He also proposed stricter educational requirements for people seeking elective office. Aspirants, he said, should be able to demonstrate that they attended primary and secondary school. People who forge academic certificates or falsely claim to have the required educational background should face legal consequences, he added.

Obi said the law should apply equally to citizens and public officeholders, regardless of their status. He described his political ambition as an effort to build a country with functioning institutions, respect for electoral rules and no one above the law. The proposals would require legal and institutional measures to put them into effect, while any changes to candidate eligibility rules would need to comply with Nigeria’s constitutional and electoral framework.
